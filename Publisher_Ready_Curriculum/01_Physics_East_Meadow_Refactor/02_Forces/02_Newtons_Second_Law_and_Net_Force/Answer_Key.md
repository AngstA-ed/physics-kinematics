## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) **a = F_net / m = 12 N / 4 kg = 3 m/s².** (Equivalently F_net = m·a → 12 = 4·a → a = 3 m/s².)
(b) **a = 12 N / 8 kg = 1.5 m/s².**
(c) **The acceleration was cut in half** (3 → 1.5 m/s²) because doubling the mass — with the same net force — halves the acceleration; acceleration is inversely proportional to mass (a = F_net / m).

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) 3 m/s² with correct set-up and units; (b) 1.5 m/s²; (c) must state acceleration halved AND give the reason (more mass, same force → less acceleration). 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1.
