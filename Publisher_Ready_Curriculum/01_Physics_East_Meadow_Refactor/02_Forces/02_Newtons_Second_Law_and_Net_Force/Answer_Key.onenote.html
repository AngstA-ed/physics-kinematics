Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. a = F_net / m = 6 N / 2 kg = 3 m/s². · Rubric: correct equation set-up AND answer with units (m/s²) → full credit; equation only → partial. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1
  2. Cart B has twice the mass, so with the same net force its acceleration is half that of Cart A (acceleration is inversely proportional to mass). · Rubric: states “half” AND links to mass in the denominator. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1
  3. m = F_net / a = 20 N / 4 m/s² = 5 kg. · Rubric: rearranges F_net = m·a correctly AND reports 5 kg. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1

Exit Ticket Answer

  1. a = F_net / m = 12 N / 4 kg = 3 m/s². (Equivalently F_net = m·a → 12 = 4·a → a = 3 m/s².)
  2. a = 12 N / 8 kg = 1.5 m/s².
  3. The acceleration was cut in half (3 → 1.5 m/s²) because doubling the mass — with the same net force — halves the acceleration; acceleration is inversely proportional to mass (a = F_net / m).

Tolerance/rubric: (a) 3 m/s² with correct set-up and units; (b) 1.5 m/s²; (c) must state acceleration halved AND give the reason (more mass, same force → less acceleration). 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1.
