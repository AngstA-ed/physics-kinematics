## Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This unit develops Newton's three laws and the common forces that act in everyday and engineered systems. It builds directly on Kinematics (velocity and acceleration as changes in motion) and sets up Momentum & Impulse and Energy.

| # | Lesson | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Newton's First Law and Inertia | Motion persists unless a net force acts; inertia scales with mass |
| 02 | Newton's Second Law and Net Force | Fnet = m·a — net force causes acceleration |
| 03 | Newton's Third Law (Action and Reaction) | Forces come in equal, opposite pairs acting on different objects |
| 04 | Friction | Static vs. kinetic friction; Ff = μ·FN |
| 05 | Centripetal Force and Circular Motion | Center-directed net force; Fc = m·v²/r |
| 06 | Universal Gravitation | Fg = G·m₁·m₂/r²; inverse-square pattern |

---

## Strategy Rotation

- **HOCHMAN** (literacy): Lessons 02, 06 — Because/But/So expansion and appositives on the key relationships (Fnet = m·a, the inverse-square law).
- **ACTIVE LEARNING**: Lessons 03, 05 — force-pair hunt / station work and the predict-and-test stopper-on-a-string.
- **BTC** (Building Thinking Classrooms): Lesson 04 — random groups + vertical non-permanent surfaces on a friction thin-slice.
- **RESTORATIVE CIRCLE**: Lesson 01 — unit-opening circle ("hard to start, hard to stop").

SEL (Opening Connection + Closing Reflection), ELL/SPED supports, and a Curated Resources block appear in every lesson.
