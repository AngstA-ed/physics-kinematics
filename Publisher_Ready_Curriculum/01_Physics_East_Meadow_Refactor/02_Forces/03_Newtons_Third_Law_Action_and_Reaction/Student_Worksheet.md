## Phenomenon

Two students sit facing each other in rolling chairs, soles of their shoes touching. On a count, **only one** of them pushes. Both students roll apart.

**Driving question:** If only one person pushed, why did *both* people move?

## Make It Make Sense

1. You walk forward across the floor. Name the action–reaction pair and state which object each force acts on.
2. A rocket has no air to push against in space. How does it still speed up? Name the force pair.
3. A small student and a large student push off in rolling chairs. The forces on each other are equal — so why does the smaller student roll away faster?
