## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. The distance **doubled** while the masses stayed the same, so by the inverse-square law the force is divided by 2² = 4. New force = 12 N ÷ 4 = **3 N**. · *Rubric:* divides by 4 (not 2) AND correct value → full credit. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-4
2. **Mass** is the amount of matter you have — it does not change when you travel to the Moon. **Weight** is the gravitational *force* on that mass (Fg = mg), and the Moon's smaller mass produces a smaller g, so you weigh about 1/6 as much while your mass stays the same. · *Rubric:* defines mass as matter (unchanged) AND weight as gravitational force (smaller). · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-4
3. **Yes — the Moon pulls back on Earth with an equal and opposite force.** Gravitational forces always come in equal pairs, no matter how different the two masses are. · *Rubric:* states the pull is equal (not smaller). · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-4

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) Distance **doubled**, masses unchanged, so divide by 2² = 4. New force = 8 N ÷ 4 = **2 N**. (Inverse-square law.)
(b) **No.** Mass is the unchanging amount of matter in each satellite. Moving the satellites apart changes only the gravitational *force* between them, not their mass.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) must divide by 4 (not 2) AND give 2 N — show the 2² reasoning; (b) must distinguish mass (unchanged) from force (changed). 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS2-4.
