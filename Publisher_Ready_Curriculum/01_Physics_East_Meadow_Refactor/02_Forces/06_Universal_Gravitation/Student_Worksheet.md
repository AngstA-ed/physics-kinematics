## Phenomenon

Earth's gravity reaches all the way out to the Moon — it is exactly what holds the Moon in orbit. Yet the Moon never falls down onto Earth, and astronauts circling Earth float as if there were no gravity at all.

**Driving question:** What decides how strongly two objects pull on each other through gravity?

## Make It Make Sense

1. Two objects pull on each other with a force of 12 N. Their masses stay the same, but you double the distance between them. What is the new force?
2. Your mass is the same on Earth and on the Moon, but you would weigh about 1/6 as much on the Moon. Explain how both statements can be true.
3. Earth pulls on the Moon. Does the Moon pull back on Earth? Is that pull bigger, smaller, or equal? Explain.
