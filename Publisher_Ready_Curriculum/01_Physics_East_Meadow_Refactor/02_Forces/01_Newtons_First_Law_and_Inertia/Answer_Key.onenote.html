Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. The table pushes up on the book (the normal force) with the same size as gravity pulling down. The two forces are balanced, so the net force is zero and the book stays at rest. · Rubric: names the normal force AND states net force = 0 → full credit. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1
  2. The net force is zero (equal and opposite pushes cancel), so the box stays at rest — or, if already moving, keeps moving at constant velocity. · Rubric: net force = 0 AND correct motion outcome. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1
  3. The loaded backpack has more mass, therefore more inertia, so it takes a larger force to produce the same change in motion. · Rubric: links mass → inertia → larger force. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1

Exit Ticket Answer

  1. Zero net force. Constant speed in a straight line = constant velocity = no change in motion, which means the forces are balanced.
  2. Friction (between puck and ice) plus a small amount of air resistance.
  3. It would keep moving forever at the same velocity — with no net force, there is nothing to change its motion (Newton’s First Law).

Tolerance/rubric: (a) must state zero AND justify via constant velocity; (b) friction (air resistance optional); (c) “constant velocity forever” + “no net force.” 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1.
