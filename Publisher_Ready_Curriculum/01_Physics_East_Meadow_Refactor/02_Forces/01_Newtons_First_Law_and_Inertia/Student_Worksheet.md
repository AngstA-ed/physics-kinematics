## Phenomenon

Your teacher pulls a cloth out from under a stack of cups — and the cups barely move. In a video from the International Space Station, a tool let go in mid-air just floats in place.

## Make It Make Sense

1. A book lies still on a table. Gravity pulls it down. What second force acts on it, and why doesn't the book move?
2. Two students push a box from opposite sides with equal force. What is the net force, and what does the box do?
3. Why is it harder to get a fully loaded backpack moving than an empty one?

## Exit Ticket

A hockey puck slides across the ice at a steady speed in a straight line.

(a) What is the net force on the puck? Explain.

(b) Name the force that eventually slows it down.

(c) On a frictionless rink with no air, what would the puck do, and why?
