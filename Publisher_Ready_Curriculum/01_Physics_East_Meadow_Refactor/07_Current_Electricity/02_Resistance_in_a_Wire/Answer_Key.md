## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. Resistance is proportional to length, so doubling the length **doubles** the resistance (× 2). · *Rubric:* states direct proportion AND factor of 2. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6
2. Resistance is inversely proportional to area, so doubling the area **halves** the resistance (× ½). · *Rubric:* states inverse proportion AND factor of ½. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6
3. **Copper** makes the better connecting wire because it has **lower resistivity**, so it resists current less and wastes less energy as heat. · *Rubric:* names copper AND links to lower resistivity. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) Wire B has **twice (2×) the resistance** of Wire A, because resistance is proportional to length.
(b) Wire C has **half (½) the resistance** of Wire A, because resistance is inversely proportional to cross-sectional area.
(c) The **resistivity (ρ)** of the material. *(Also accept temperature as a property affecting resistance.)*
