Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. Each branch has the full 8 V, so each branch current = 8 ÷ 4 = 2 A; total = 2 + 2 = 4 A. · Rubric: full voltage on each branch AND correct branch/total currents. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6
  2. 1/R_total = 1/4 + 1/4 = 2/4 = 1/2, so R_total = 2 Ω (smaller than either branch). · Rubric: correct reciprocal sum AND inverts to 2 Ω. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6
  3. The total current goes up, because adding a parallel branch lowers the equivalent resistance, so the battery delivers more total current. · Rubric: “up” AND links to lower R_total. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6

Exit Ticket Answer

  1. Each resistor has the full 12 V across it (parallel branches share the source voltage).
  2. I₄ = V ÷ R = 12 ÷ 4 = 3 A; I₆ = 12 ÷ 6 = 2 A.
  3. Total current = 3 + 2 = 5 A. Equivalent resistance: 1/R_total = 1/4 + 1/6 = 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12, so R_total = 12/5 = 2.4 Ω (check: 12 V ÷ 5 A = 2.4 Ω ✓).

Tolerance/rubric: (a) 12 V each; (b) 3 A and 2 A; (c) 5 A total and 2.4 Ω. 1 point each. Accept R_total ≈ 2.4 Ω. NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6.

Closing Reflection (rubric)

Formative, not graded. Award full credit for any honest surprise that contrasts parallel with series (common: “the voltage is the same on every branch, not split” or “the total resistance got smaller, not bigger”).
