## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) **R = V ÷ I = 6 V ÷ 2 A = 3 Ω.** (Must show V = I·R rearranged.)
(b) Same resistor (3 Ω), new voltage 12 V: **I = V ÷ R = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 A.**
(c) The slope of a V-vs-I graph represents the **resistance** of the circuit element.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) 3 Ω with work; (b) 4 A; (c) "slope = resistance." 1 point each. Accept correct units stated as Ω and A. NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6.
