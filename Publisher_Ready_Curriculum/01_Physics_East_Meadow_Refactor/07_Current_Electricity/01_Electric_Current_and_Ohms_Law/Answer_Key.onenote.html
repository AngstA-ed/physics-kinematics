Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. R = V ÷ I = 12 V ÷ 3 A = 4 Ω. · Rubric: correct rearrangement of V = I·R AND correct value with unit. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6
  2. I = V ÷ R = 24 V ÷ 4 Ω = 6 A. · Rubric: solves I = V/R; unit amperes. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6
  3. The slope represents the resistance, in ohms (Ω) — a slope of 3 means R = 3 Ω. · Rubric: names resistance AND gives ohms. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6

Exit Ticket Answer

  1. R = V ÷ I = 6 V ÷ 2 A = 3 Ω. (Must show V = I·R rearranged.)
  2. Same resistor (3 Ω), new voltage 12 V: I = V ÷ R = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 A.
  3. The slope of a V-vs-I graph represents the resistance of the circuit element.

Tolerance/rubric: (a) 3 Ω with work; (b) 4 A; (c) “slope = resistance.” 1 point each. Accept correct units stated as Ω and A. NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6.
