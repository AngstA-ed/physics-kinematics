## Guided Notes

**What flows, and what pushes it.**

- **Current (I)** is the flow of electric __________ through a wire. It equals charge ÷ __________, and is measured in __________ (A).
- **Voltage (V)**, also called potential difference, is the __________ that drives the charge around the loop. It is measured in __________ (V).
- **Resistance (R)** is how much a material __________ the flow. It is measured in __________ (Ω).
- Charge needs a __________ (closed) loop to flow — break the loop and the current __________.

**Ohm's Law.**

> **V = I · R**     (voltage = current × resistance)

Rearranged:  I = V ÷ __________     and     R = V ÷ __________.

**Reading the V-vs-I graph.** When you plot V (up) against I (across) for one resistor, the points form a straight __________ through the origin. The __________ of that line equals the __________.

**Power (extension).** The electrical power delivered is **P = V·I = I²·R**, measured in watts (W).

## Worked Example

**Problem.** A resistor has 9 V across it and a current of 3 A flowing through it. (a) Find its resistance. (b) If the voltage is raised to 18 V, find the new current.

**Solution.**
1. Write Ohm's Law: V = I·R, so R = V ÷ I = 9 V ÷ 3 A = __________ Ω.
2. Same resistor, new voltage: I = V ÷ R = 18 V ÷ __________ Ω = __________ A.
3. Doubling the voltage __________ the current, because R stayed the same.
