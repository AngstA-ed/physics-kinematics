Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This unit develops the physics of moving charge: what current and voltage are, how Ohm’s Law (V = I·R) relates them through resistance, how resistance arises in a wire, how resistors combine in series and parallel circuits, and how current and magnetism interact through electromagnetic induction. It builds on the energy ideas of HS-PS3 and the charge ideas from Electrostatics, and it closes with the field/energy connection that anchors generators and motors.

# Lesson Core idea
01 Electric Current and Ohm’s Law Current = charge ÷ time; voltage drives current; V = I·R; slope of V–I is R
02 Resistance in a Wire R rises with length and resistivity, falls with cross-sectional area; temperature matters
03 Series Circuits One path; same current everywhere; R_total = R₁ + R₂ + …; voltage drops add
04 Parallel Circuits Multiple paths; same voltage per branch; currents add; 1/R_total = Σ 1/R
05 Electromagnetism (The Induction Junction) Current makes a magnetic field; a changing field induces current; generators & motors

Double-period days (every other day) let the investigation phases of Lessons 03–05 run long with the PhET/Javalab labs and the hands-on induction station.

Strategy Rotation

  • HOCHMAN (literacy): Lesson 01 — Because/But/So expansion on the V–I–R relationship (the “but” clause carries the resistance idea).
  • BTC (Building Thinking Classrooms): Lesson 02 — visibly random groups at vertical non-permanent surfaces on a resistance thin-slice (length vs. area).
  • ACTIVE LEARNING: Lessons 03, 04, 05 — predict-measure-reconcile at PhET stations (series/parallel) and a hands-on NYS induction investigation.

SEL (Opening Connection + Closing Reflection), ELL/SPED supports, and a Curated Resources block appear in every lesson.
