## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. R_total = 1 + 2 + 3 = **6 Ω**; I = V ÷ R_total = 12 ÷ 6 = **2 A** (everywhere). · *Rubric:* correct sum AND I = V/R_total. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6
2. V across 3 Ω = I·R = 2 × 3 = **6 V**; V across 5 Ω = 2 × 5 = **10 V**. · *Rubric:* uses the common 2 A current with V = I·R for each. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6
3. The current goes **down**, because adding a series resistor raises R_total and I = V ÷ R_total. · *Rubric:* "down" AND links to larger R_total. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) **R_total = 4 Ω + 8 Ω = 12 Ω.**
(b) **I = V ÷ R_total = 24 ÷ 12 = 2 A.**
(c) Across the 8 Ω: **V₈ = I·R = 2 × 8 = 16 V.** Across the 4 Ω: V₄ = 2 × 4 = 8 V. Check: **16 + 8 = 24 V** = the source. ✓

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) 12 Ω; (b) 2 A; (c) 16 V with a correct sum-check to 24 V. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS3-6.
