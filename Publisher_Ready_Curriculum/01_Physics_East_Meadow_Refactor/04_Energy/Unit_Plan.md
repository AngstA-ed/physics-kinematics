## Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This unit develops energy as the unifying currency of physics. It builds on Forces (work is done by a force through a displacement) and Kinematics (speed and height), and it sets up later units on momentum, electricity, and thermodynamics. Students first quantify the *transfer* of energy (work, power), then the two macroscopic *stores* of mechanical energy (kinetic = motion, potential = position), then the law that ties them together (conservation), and finally apply it all to real energy-conversion devices (efficiency and engineering design).

| # | Lesson | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Work | W = F·d; only the force component along the displacement does work; the joule |
| 02 | Power | P = W/t = Fv; the watt; same work faster = more power |
| 03 | Kinetic Energy | KE = ½mv²; energy of motion depends on v² |
| 04 | Gravitational Potential Energy | PE = mgh; stored energy relative to a reference height |
| 05 | Conservation of Energy | Mechanical energy KE + PE is constant without friction; PE ↔ KE |
| 06 | Energy in Systems: Wheels to Watts | Energy transfer, useful vs. wasted, efficiency = useful ÷ input; design/refine a converter |

Reference-table formulas used this unit: W = F·d; P = W/t = Fv; KE = ½mv²; PE = mgh; g ≈ 9.81 m/s².

## Strategy Rotation

- **HOCHMAN** (literacy): Lessons 01, 04 — appositive definitions and Because/But/So on W = F·d (the zero-work case) and PE = mgh (the reference-height idea).
- **ACTIVE LEARNING**: Lessons 02, 05 — the stair-climb personal-power lab and the PhET Energy Skate Park predict-and-test with student-read energy bar graphs.
- **BTC** (Building Thinking Classrooms): Lessons 03, 06 — random groups + vertical non-permanent surfaces on the thin-sliced KE = ½mv² (v² discovery) and the Wheels-to-Watts design-and-refine task.

SEL (Opening Connection + Closing Reflection), ELL/SPED supports, and a Curated Resources block appear in every lesson.
