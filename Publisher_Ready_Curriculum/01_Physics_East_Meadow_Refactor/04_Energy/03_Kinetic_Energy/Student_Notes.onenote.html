Worked Example

Problem. A 1200 kg car is moving at 25 m/s. What is its kinetic energy?

Solution. 1. List knowns: m = __________ kg, v = __________ m/s. 2. Formula: KE = ½mv² = ½ × __________ × (__________)². 3. Square the speed first: (25)² = __________. 4. Multiply: KE = ½ × 1200 × 625 = __________ J.
