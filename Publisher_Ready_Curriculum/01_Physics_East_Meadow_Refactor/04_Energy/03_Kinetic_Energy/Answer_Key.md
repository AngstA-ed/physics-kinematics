## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. KE = ½mv² = ½ × 2.0 kg × (3.0 m/s)² = ½ × 2.0 × 9.0 = **9.0 J**. · *Rubric:* squares the speed first AND reports 9.0 J. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-2
2. Car B's speed is double car A's. Since KE ∝ v², doubling v gives **four times** the kinetic energy. (Check: A = ½m(15²) = ½m(225); B = ½m(30²) = ½m(900); 900/225 = 4.) · *Rubric:* answer is 4× AND justified by v². · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-2
3. Doubling the speed does **not** double the energy — KE = ½mv² has speed *squared*, so doubling v gives 2² = **four times** the kinetic energy. · *Rubric:* corrects "double" to "four times" using the v² term. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-2

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) KE = ½mv² = ½ × 1500 kg × (10 m/s)² = ½ × 1500 × 100 = **75,000 J** (7.5 × 10⁴ J).
(b) KE = ½ × 1500 × (20)² = ½ × 1500 × 400 = **300,000 J** (3.0 × 10⁵ J) — a factor of **4×** (because speed doubled and KE ∝ v²).
(c) Kinetic energy depends on v², so doubling the speed quadruples the energy of motion; about **four times** as much work (and stopping distance) is needed to bring it to rest.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) shows ½mv² and 75,000 J; (b) 300,000 J AND the 4× factor; (c) ties the 4× difficulty to v². 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS3-2.
