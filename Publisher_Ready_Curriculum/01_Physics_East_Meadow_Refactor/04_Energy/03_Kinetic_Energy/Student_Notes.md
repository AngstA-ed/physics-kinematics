## Guided Notes

**Big idea:** Kinetic energy is the energy an object has because of its __________.

- Reference-table formula: **KE = ½mv²**, where m is __________ (kg) and v is __________ (m/s). Unit: __________ (J).
- Kinetic energy depends on **mass** to the __________ power.
- Kinetic energy depends on **speed** __________ (the v² term).
- Because speed is squared: doubling v multiplies KE by __________; tripling v multiplies KE by __________.

## Worked Example

**Problem.** A 1200 kg car is moving at 25 m/s. What is its kinetic energy?

**Solution.**
1. List knowns: m = __________ kg, v = __________ m/s.
2. Formula: KE = ½mv² = ½ × __________ × (__________)².
3. Square the speed first: (25)² = __________.
4. Multiply: KE = ½ × 1200 × 625 = __________ J.
