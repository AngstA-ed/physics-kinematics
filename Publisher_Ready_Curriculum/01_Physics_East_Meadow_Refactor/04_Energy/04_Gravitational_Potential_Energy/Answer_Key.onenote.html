  3. PE = mgh uses h measured from a reference. The same object has different h (and thus different PE) depending on where we call “zero,” because potential energy describes relative position, not an absolute property. · Rubric: states PE is relative to a chosen reference. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-2

Exit Ticket Answer

  1. PE = mgh = 500 kg × 9.81 m/s² × 30 m = 147,150 J (≈ 1.47 × 10⁵ J) relative to the ground.
  2. PE = 500 × 9.81 × 15 = 73,575 J (≈ 7.36 × 10⁴ J) — exactly half, because PE is proportional to height.
  3. Measuring from a platform halfway up makes h smaller (15 m instead of 30 m), so the PE would be smaller; PE depends on the reference height we choose.
