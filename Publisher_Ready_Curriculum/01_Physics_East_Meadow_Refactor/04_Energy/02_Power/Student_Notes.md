## Guided Notes

**Big idea:** Power is the __________ at which work is done — how __________ energy is transferred.

- Reference-table formula: **P = W/t = Fv**, where W is __________ (J) and t is __________ (s).
- The unit of power is the __________ (W). One watt = one __________ per __________ (1 W = 1 J/s).
- **Energy** (joules) is the amount transferred; **power** (watts) is how __________ it is transferred.
- Same work in __________ time → __________ power.
- Rearranged: energy used = P × t, so a 100 W device for 5 s uses __________ J.

**Slope of a work-vs-time graph = __________.** A steeper line means __________ power.

## Worked Example

**Problem.** A 600 N student climbs a 5.0 m staircase in 4.0 s. What is the student's power output?

**Solution.**
1. Find the work: W = F·d = __________ N × __________ m = __________ J.
2. Use the power formula: P = W/t = __________ J ÷ __________ s.
3. Compute: P = __________ W.
4. Units check: joules ÷ seconds = __________ = watts. ✓
