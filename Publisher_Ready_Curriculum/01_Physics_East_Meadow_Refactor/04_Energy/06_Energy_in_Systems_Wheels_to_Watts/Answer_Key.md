2. Incandescent: 5% of 100 J = **5 J** of useful light; LED: 40% of 100 J = **40 J** of useful light. The rest (95 J and 60 J) becomes **heat**. · *Rubric:* both useful values AND "rest becomes heat." · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-3
3. No real device is 100% efficient because **friction** and electrical **resistance** always convert some input energy into **heat** (and sound), so useful output is always less than input. · *Rubric:* names friction/resistance → heat → useful < input. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-3

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) Efficiency = useful ÷ input = 300 J ÷ 400 J = **0.75 = 75%**.
(b) The other **100 J** became **heat** (friction in bearings, resistance in the wires) and a little sound. It did **not** disappear — energy is conserved; it just left the useful path.
(c) **Any reasonable refinement that reduces wasted energy**, e.g.: better/oiled bearings or smoother gearing (less friction), thicker/lower-resistance wire (less resistive heating), or a better-matched load. It works by shifting energy from the wasted (heat) path to the useful (electrical) output, raising useful ÷ input.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) shows the ratio and 75%; (b) "100 J → heat, not destroyed"; (c) a refinement that reduces friction/resistance AND a correct justification. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS3-3.
