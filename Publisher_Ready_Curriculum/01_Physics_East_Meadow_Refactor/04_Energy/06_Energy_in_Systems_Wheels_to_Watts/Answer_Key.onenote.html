Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. Efficiency = useful ÷ input = 150 J ÷ 200 J = 0.75 = 75%. · Rubric: correct ratio AND 75%. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-3
  2. Incandescent: 5% of 100 J = 5 J of useful light; LED: 40% of 100 J = 40 J of useful light. The rest (95 J and 60 J) becomes heat. · Rubric: both useful values AND “rest becomes heat.” · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-3
  3. No real device is 100% efficient because friction and electrical resistance always convert some input energy into heat (and sound), so useful output is always less than input. · Rubric: names friction/resistance → heat → useful < input. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-3

Exit Ticket Answer

  1. Efficiency = useful ÷ input = 300 J ÷ 400 J = 0.75 = 75%.
  2. The other 100 J became heat (friction in bearings, resistance in the wires) and a little sound. It did not disappear — energy is conserved; it just left the useful path.
  3. Any reasonable refinement that reduces wasted energy, e.g.: better/oiled bearings or smoother gearing (less friction), thicker/lower-resistance wire (less resistive heating), or a better-matched load. It works by shifting energy from the wasted (heat) path to the useful (electrical) output, raising useful ÷ input.

Tolerance/rubric: (a) shows the ratio and 75%; (b) “100 J → heat, not destroyed”; (c) a refinement that reduces friction/resistance AND a correct justification. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS3-3.
