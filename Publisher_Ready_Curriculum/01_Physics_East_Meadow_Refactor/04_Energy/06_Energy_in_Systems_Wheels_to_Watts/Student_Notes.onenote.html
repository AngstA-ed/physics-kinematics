Problem. A bike generator takes in 500 J of pedaling energy and delivers 350 J of useful electrical energy. Find its efficiency and the wasted energy.

Solution. 1. Efficiency = useful ÷ input = __________ ÷ __________ . 2. Compute: efficiency = __________ = __________ %. 3. Wasted energy = input − useful = 500 − 350 = __________ J. 4. That wasted energy became __________ ; it was not destroyed (energy is conserved).
