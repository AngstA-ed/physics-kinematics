## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. W = F·d = 200 N × 3.0 m = **600 J**. The force points the same direction the crate moves, so all of it does work. · *Rubric:* correct substitution AND 600 J with unit → full credit. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-1
2. **Zero work.** Work requires a displacement (W = F·d). The tray's displacement is zero, so W = 0 regardless of how heavy it is or how long it is held. · *Rubric:* states W = 0 AND justifies via d = 0. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-1
3. While carrying, the supporting force points **up** but the box's displacement is **sideways** (horizontal). The force is **perpendicular** to the motion, so the component of force along the displacement is zero — therefore **zero work** on the box. Tired muscles do not equal physics work. · *Rubric:* identifies perpendicular force/motion AND concludes W = 0. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-1

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) W = F·d = 250 N × 8.0 m = **2000 J** (2.0 × 10³ J).
(b) **Zero work.** During the 10 s of holding, the bucket does not move (d = 0), so W = F·d = 0.
(c) **Zero work on the tray.** The support force is vertical (up) while the tray moves horizontally; the force is perpendicular to the displacement, so it does no work.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) must show F·d and give 2000 J; (b) zero + "no displacement"; (c) zero + "force perpendicular to motion." 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS3-1.
