## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. PE lost = mgh = 2.0 kg × 9.81 m/s² × 5.0 m ≈ 98 J. By conservation (PE lost = KE gained), KE just before landing ≈ **98 J**. · *Rubric:* computes mgh AND equates to KE. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-1
2. At the bottom the height is least, so **PE is minimum**; all of it has transformed into motion, so **KE is maximum**. The two trade off. · *Rubric:* PE minimum AND KE maximum at the bottom. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-2
3. Energy is conserved, but friction and air resistance **transform** some mechanical energy into **heat** each swing. The mechanical total (KE + PE) decreases, so the pendulum returns a little lower until it stops. · *Rubric:* names transformation to heat AND that mechanical energy decreases. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-1

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) PE = mgh = 0.50 kg × 9.81 m/s² × 2.0 m = **9.81 J** (≈ 9.8 J).
(b) By conservation of energy on a frictionless track, PE lost = KE gained, so KE at the bottom = **9.81 J** (≈ 9.8 J). All the PE became KE.
(c) **Less.** Friction transforms some mechanical energy into **heat** (and a little sound), so less KE reaches the bottom; total energy is still conserved.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) shows mgh and ≈ 9.8 J; (b) ≈ 9.8 J via PE lost = KE gained; (c) "less" + "energy goes to heat." 1 point each. Accept g = 9.8 (≈ 9.8 J). NYSSLS: HS-PS3-1.
