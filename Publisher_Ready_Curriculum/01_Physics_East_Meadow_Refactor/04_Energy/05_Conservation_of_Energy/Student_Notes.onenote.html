Worked Example

Problem. A 0.50 kg cart starts at rest at the top of a frictionless 2.0 m track. Find its KE and speed at the bottom. (g ≈ 9.81 m/s²)

Solution. 1. PE at top: PE = mgh = 0.50 × 9.81 × 2.0 = __________ J. 2. Conservation: PE lost = KE gained, so KE at bottom = __________ J. 3. Find speed from KE = ½mv²: __________ = ½ × 0.50 × v². 4. Solve: v² = __________ , so v = __________ m/s.
