Worked Example

Problem. A metal sphere is charged by induction using a rod that is +8 units. After the procedure, what sign is the sphere’s net charge, and why?

Solution. 1. The + rod is brought near; it __________ the sphere (− on the near side, + on the far side). 2. Grounding lets the rod-repelled charge (the __________ charges) escape to the ground. 3. Remove the ground, then the rod → the sphere keeps the charge __________ to the rod. 4. The rod was +, so the sphere ends up __________ — opposite, with no contact.
