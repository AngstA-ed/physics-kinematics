## Guided Notes

**Big idea:** A charge fills the space around it with an **electric field** — a __________ per unit charge at every point.

- Formula: E = __________ / __________, with units __________ (newtons per coulomb).
- We find its direction with a tiny **positive test charge**: the field points the way that charge is __________.

- Near a **negative** charge, the field points __________ (toward).

## Worked Example

**Problem.** A field of **500 N/C** points east at a point. What force does it exert on a charge of **+2.0 × 10⁻⁶ C** placed there, and in which direction?

**Solution.**
1. Rearrange E = Fₑ / q to solve for force: Fₑ = __________ · __________.
2. Substitute: Fₑ = (500 N/C)(2.0 × 10⁻⁶ C) = __________ N.
3. Result: Fₑ ≈ **1.0 × 10⁻³ N**.
4. The charge is __________, so it is pushed in the __________ direction the field points → **east**.
