3. A positive test charge is the agreed **convention** for field direction (the field points the way a + charge is pushed). It must be **small** so it does not disturb (move or distort) the field it is measuring. · *Rubric:* convention/direction AND "small so it doesn't disturb the field." · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-5

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) E = Fₑ / q = 0.012 N / (4.0 × 10⁻⁶ C) = **3.0 × 10³ N/C** (3000 N/C).
(b) The arrows point **inward — toward the negative charge** (a + test charge is attracted to it).
(c) A test charge is a **tiny positive charge** used to probe a field's direction and strength; it must be small so it **does not disturb the field** it is measuring.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) 3.0 × 10³ N/C with units (accept 3000 N/C); (b) "toward / inward"; (c) "small positive" + "doesn't disturb the field." 1 point each (part a worth 2: setup + answer). NYSSLS: HS-PS3-5.
