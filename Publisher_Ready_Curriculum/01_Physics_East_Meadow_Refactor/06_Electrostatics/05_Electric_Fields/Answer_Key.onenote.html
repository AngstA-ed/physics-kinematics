Exit Ticket Answer

  1. E = Fₑ / q = 0.012 N / (4.0 × 10⁻⁶ C) = 3.0 × 10³ N/C (3000 N/C).
  2. The arrows point inward — toward the negative charge (a + test charge is attracted to it).
  3. A test charge is a tiny positive charge used to probe a field’s direction and strength; it must be small so it does not disturb the field it is measuring.

Tolerance/rubric: (a) 3.0 × 10³ N/C with units (accept 3000 N/C); (b) “toward / inward”; (c) “small positive” + “doesn’t disturb the field.” 1 point each (part a worth 2: setup + answer). NYSSLS: HS-PS3-5.
