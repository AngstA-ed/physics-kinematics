## Worked Example

**Problem.** A rod carrying a charge of **−10 units** is touched to an identical neutral metal sphere. The charge shares equally between the two metals. What charge ends up on the sphere, and what sign is it?

**Solution.**
1. The rod is a conductor and the sphere is a conductor, so charge can __________ between them.
2. Total charge = −10 units, shared __________ between two identical metals → −5 units each.
3. The sphere ends up with __________ units — the __________ sign as the rod (conduction shares charge, it does not reverse it).
