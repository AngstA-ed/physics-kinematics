## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. The sphere becomes **negative** — the same sign as the rod. This is **charging by conduction**: direct contact lets some of the rod's charge spread onto the sphere. · *Rubric:* "negative" AND "conduction." · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-5
2. Your body and the ground form a conductor to the Earth, so the excess charge spreads off the object **into the ground** through you, leaving it neutral. · *Rubric:* charge leaves through you to ground/Earth. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-5
3. **No.** The wooden block is an **insulator**, so even at the contact point the charge cannot spread through it; only the touched spot might hold a little charge. · *Rubric:* identifies wood as an insulator AND says charge can't spread. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-5

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) Copper wire = **conductor**; rubber band = **insulator**; aluminum foil = **conductor**; glass rod = **insulator**.
(b) The sphere is **negative**, charged by **conduction** (it ends with the same sign as the rod).
(c) The doorknob is a **conductor**, so charge moves freely onto it; the plastic cup is an **insulator**, so charge cannot flow into it.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) all four correct = 1 pt (½ for 2–3 correct); (b) "negative" + "conduction"; (c) contrasts conductor vs. insulator. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS3-5.
