## Guided Notes

**Coulomb's law:** Fₑ = k · q₁ · q₂ / r²

- Fₑ = the __________ force, in newtons (N)
- q₁, q₂ = the two __________, in coulombs (C)
- r = the __________ between the charges, in meters (m)
- k = __________ × 10⁹ N·m²/C² (Coulomb constant)

**Patterns:**

- Force is __________ to each charge → double a charge, the force __________.
- Force depends on distance __________ (squared) → double r, force becomes __________; triple r, force becomes __________.

## Worked Example

**Problem.** Two charges of **+2.0 × 10⁻⁶ C** each sit **0.30 m** apart. Find the electrostatic force, and state whether it is attractive or repulsive.

**Solution.**
1. Write the formula: Fₑ = k · q₁ · q₂ / r².
2. Substitute: Fₑ = (8.99 × 10⁹)(2.0 × 10⁻⁶)(2.0 × 10⁻⁶) / (0.30)².
3. Numerator: (8.99 × 10⁹)(4.0 × 10⁻¹²) = __________ ≈ 3.6 × 10⁻² N·m².
4. Divide by r² = (0.30)² = 0.090 m²: Fₑ ≈ __________ ≈ **0.40 N**.
5. Both charges are positive, so the force is __________ (repulsive).

**Check the pattern:** if r doubles to 0.60 m, r² ×4, so Fₑ → 0.40 / 4 = __________ N.
