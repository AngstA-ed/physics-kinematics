## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. They feel the **same-size force** — equal in magnitude and opposite in direction (Newton's third law). The smaller charge does not feel a smaller force; Fₑ uses the *product* q₁·q₂, which is the same for both. · *Rubric:* "equal/same force" + Newton's third law or "same product." · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-4
2. The force goes up **nine times** (×9). Each charge tripled multiplies the force by 3, and 3 × 3 = 9. · *Rubric:* "×9" with the 3 × 3 reasoning. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-4
3. **Gravitation** (Fg = G·m₁·m₂ / r²). The shared inverse-square shape shows nature reuses the same mathematical Pattern for very different forces. · *Rubric:* names gravitation AND the Patterns idea. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-4

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) Fₑ = (8.99 × 10⁹)(3.0 × 10⁻⁶)(3.0 × 10⁻⁶) / (0.20)²
   = (8.99 × 10⁹)(9.0 × 10⁻¹²) / 0.040
   = (8.09 × 10⁻²) / 0.040 ≈ **2.0 N**. **Repulsive** (both charges positive).
(b) Tripling the distance multiplies r² by 9, so the force is divided by 9: 2.0 / 9 ≈ **0.22 N**.
(c) **Newton's law of universal gravitation**, Fg = G·m₁·m₂ / r² (the inverse-square Pattern, HS-PS2-4).

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) accept 2.0 N ± 0.1 (≈ 2.02 N) AND "repulsive"; (b) "÷9" or ≈ 0.22 N; (c) gravitation. 1 point each (part a worth 2: setup + answer). NYSSLS: HS-PS2-4.

**Worked detail for (a):** q₁·q₂ = (3.0 × 10⁻⁶)² = 9.0 × 10⁻¹² C². Times k: 8.99 × 10⁹ × 9.0 × 10⁻¹² = 8.09 × 10⁻² N·m². Divide by r² = 0.040 m²: 8.09 × 10⁻² / 0.040 = 2.02 N.
