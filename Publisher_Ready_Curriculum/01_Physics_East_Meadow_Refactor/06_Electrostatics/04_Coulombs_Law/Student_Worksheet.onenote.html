Phenomenon

Your teacher drags two charged spheres in the PhET “Coulomb’s Law” simulation. Doubling one charge doubles the force — no surprise. But halving the distance quadruples the force, and the readout looks just like the gravity behavior from the Forces unit.

Driving question: How exactly does the force between two charges depend on the charges and on the distance between them?

Notice & Wonder

I notice… I wonder…

Turn & Talk #1: Halving the distance made the force four times bigger, not two. What relationship between force and distance would do that?
