Worked Example

Problem. A glass rod is rubbed with silk. The rod ends up with a charge of +6 units. What charge does the silk carry, and why?

Solution. 1. Rubbing only __________ charge — it does not create it (conservation of charge). 2. The rod became +6, so it __________ 6 units of negative charge (electrons). 3. Those electrons went __________ the silk, so the silk carries a charge of __________ units. 4. Total charge before = total charge after = __________.
