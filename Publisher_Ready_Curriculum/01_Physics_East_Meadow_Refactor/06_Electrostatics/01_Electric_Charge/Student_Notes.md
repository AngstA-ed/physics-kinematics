## Guided Notes

**Big idea:** All matter is made of atoms that contain __________ charge (protons) and __________ charge (electrons).

- There are exactly __________ kinds of charge: positive and negative.
- Like charges __________ (push apart). Unlike charges __________ (pull together).
- A **neutral** object has __________ amounts of positive and negative charge.
- Charge is **quantized**: it comes in whole multiples of the elementary charge *e* = __________ × 10⁻¹⁹ C.
- Charge is **conserved**: it is never created or destroyed, only __________ from one object to another.

## Worked Example

**Problem.** A glass rod is rubbed with silk. The rod ends up with a charge of **+6 units**. What charge does the silk carry, and why?

**Solution.**
1. Rubbing only __________ charge — it does not create it (conservation of charge).
2. The rod became **+6**, so it __________ 6 units of negative charge (electrons).
3. Those electrons went __________ the silk, so the silk carries a charge of __________ units.
4. Total charge before = total charge after = __________.
