Phenomenon

A vinyl record gets scratchier every time it’s played, and a copy of a copy of a photocopy turns to mush. But you can stream the same song a million times, or copy a PDF endlessly, and the last copy is identical to the first.

Driving question: Why does a digital copy survive perfectly while an analog copy degrades?

Notice & Wonder

I notice… I wonder…

Turn & Talk #1: What is it about storing something as numbers (digital) that lets it survive copying perfectly?

Initial Model

Draw a smooth wave (analog). Now show how you would store it using only a few measured points (samples). What information do you lose? What do you gain?
