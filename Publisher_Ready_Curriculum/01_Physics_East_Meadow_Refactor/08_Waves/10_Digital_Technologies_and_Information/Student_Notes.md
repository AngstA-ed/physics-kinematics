## Guided Notes

**Analog vs. digital.**

![Analog sine wave overlaid with its sampled, quantized digital version.](figures/analog_vs_digital.png)

- **Analog** — a __________ signal that can take any value (vinyl groove, sound in air).
- **Digital** — a signal stored as __________ (sampled) values, each rounded to a level and written as __________.

**Sampling.** To digitize a wave you measure it at regular __________ and store each measurement.

![Sampling: a wave measured at regular intervals, 8 levels = 3 bits.](figures/sampling.png)

- More samples per second + more bits per sample → __________ fidelity.
- A **bit** is a single __________ or __________; 3 bits give 2³ = __________ levels.

**Why digital wins (HS-PS4-2, Stability and Change).**

- Noise on a digital signal can be __________ back to the nearest level → copies stay identical.
- Noise on an analog signal becomes part of the signal and __________ up with each copy.
- Digital is also easy to __________, compress, and error-check.

**Trade-off:** digital loses the detail __________ the samples unless you sample fast enough.
