## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. In light (transverse), the wave oscillates **perpendicular** to the direction the light travels — the electric and magnetic fields wiggle across the line of travel while the energy moves forward. · *Rubric:* states perpendicular relationship. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
2. Sound is **longitudinal**: the particles push on the next particles to pass the compression along, so there must be matter (a medium) to do the pushing. In a vacuum there are no particles to push, so sound cannot travel. · *Rubric:* links particle-pushing to needing a medium. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
3. **Transverse.** The string moves up and down (side to side) while the wave travels along the length of the string — the motion is perpendicular to the travel direction. · *Rubric:* transverse AND perpendicular justification. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) **Longitudinal.**
(b) The air molecules move **back and forth parallel** to the direction the sound travels (not across it).
(c) Bunched-up regions are **compressions**; spread-out regions are **rarefactions**.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) longitudinal; (b) parallel/back-and-forth along travel; (c) both terms correct. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1.
