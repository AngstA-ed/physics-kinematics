Phenomenon

An opera singer holds one steady note and a wine glass shatters. On a shaken rope, most hand-speeds make a messy mess — but at one special speed the rope locks into a clean, big pattern that seems to stand still.

Driving question: What is special about the one frequency that makes a clean, standing pattern?

Notice & Wonder

I notice… I wonder…

Turn & Talk #1: On the rope at the special frequency, where is there no motion at all, and where is the motion biggest?

Initial Model

Sketch the rope at the special frequency. Mark with an X every point that does not move, and a star at every point that moves the most. Predict: what happens to the number of loops if you shake faster?

Exit Ticket

  1. How many nodes are there in total (including the ends)? How many antinodes?
