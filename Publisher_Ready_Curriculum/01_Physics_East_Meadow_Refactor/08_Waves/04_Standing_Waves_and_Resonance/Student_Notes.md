## Guided Notes

**How a standing wave forms.** A wave hits a fixed end and __________ back. The returning wave __________ (interferes) with the new wave. At certain frequencies they always add the same way, making a pattern that stays in __________.

![Three standing-wave harmonics with nodes and antinodes labeled.](figures/standing_wave_harmonics.png)

- **Node** — a point that __________ moves (the waves cancel there).
- **Antinode** — a point that moves the __________.
- **Fundamental (1st harmonic)** — __________ antinode.
- **2nd harmonic** — __________ antinodes, 1 interior node.
- **3rd harmonic** — 3 antinodes, __________ interior nodes.

**Resonance.** Driving a system at one of its __________ frequencies produces a __________ (large) response. Examples: a swing pushed in rhythm, a shattering glass, a vibrating string.

---

## Worked Example

**Problem.** A standing wave on a string shows **3 full loops** between the fixed ends.

**Solution.**
1. Each loop is one antinode → there are __________ antinodes.
2. Nodes appear between loops plus at both fixed ends → __________ nodes total.
3. This is the __________ harmonic.
