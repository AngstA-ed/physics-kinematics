Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. Superposition: when two waves overlap, the total displacement at each point equals the point-by-point sum of the two waves. · Rubric: conveys point-by-point addition. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
  2. In phase → amplitudes add → 6 cm (constructive). Out of phase → amplitudes cancel → 0 cm (destructive). · Rubric: both values correct with phase reasoning. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
  3. The headphone plays a wave that is exactly out of phase with the hum. By superposition the two cancel (destructive interference), so the listener hears silence. · Rubric: out-of-phase AND cancellation. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1

Exit Ticket Answer

  1. Destructive interference.
  2. The two waves arrive out of phase — a crest of one meets a trough of the other, so they cancel.
  3. In phase → amplitudes add → 8 cm.

Tolerance/rubric: (a) destructive; (b) out of phase / crest-on-trough; (c) 8 cm. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1.
