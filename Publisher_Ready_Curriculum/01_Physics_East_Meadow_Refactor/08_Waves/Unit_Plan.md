## Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This unit develops the physics of waves — mechanical and electromagnetic — from anatomy and the wave equation through interference, the Doppler effect, the EM spectrum, geometric optics, and the analog-to-digital encoding of information. It builds on Energy (a wave transports energy) and connects outward to astronomy (redshift → expanding universe) and to information technology (digital transmission and storage).

| # | Lesson | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Wave Anatomy and Characteristics | Amplitude, wavelength, frequency, period; a wave transfers energy, not matter |
| 02 | Transverse and Longitudinal Waves | Oscillation perpendicular (light) vs. parallel (sound) to travel |
| 03 | Wave Speed, Frequency, and Wavelength | v = f·λ; at fixed speed, f and λ trade off inversely; T = 1/f |
| 04 | Standing Waves and Resonance | Interference of reflections → nodes/antinodes; resonance at natural frequencies |
| 05 | Interference (Constructive and Destructive) | Superposition; in phase adds, out of phase cancels |
| 06 | The Doppler Effect | Relative motion shifts observed frequency; light → red/blueshift |
| 07 | The Electromagnetic Spectrum | EM waves are transverse, all travel at c; radio → gamma by increasing f |
| 08 | Refraction and Snell's Law | Light bends when speed changes; n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂; total internal reflection |
| 09 | Optics (Lenses and Mirrors) | Ray diagrams; 1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ; real vs. virtual images |
| 10 | Digital Technologies and Information | Analog vs. digital; sampling/bits; digital is robust to noise |

**Reference-table equations used in this unit:** v = f·λ · T = 1/f · n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂ · 1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ

## Strategy Rotation

- **HOCHMAN** (literacy): Lessons 03, 06, 08 — Because/But/So expansion on the key causal relationships (v = f·λ trade-off; Doppler shift; refraction from a speed change).
- **ACTIVE LEARNING**: Lessons 02, 04, 07, 09 — slinky/human-wave building, harmonic hunting, EM-spectrum card sort, and hands-on ray-tracing.
- **BTC** (Building Thinking Classrooms): Lessons 05, 10 — random groups + vertical non-permanent surfaces on interference thin-slices and the analog/digital evaluation.
- **RESTORATIVE CIRCLE**: Lesson 01 — unit-opening circle ("a sound or signal that travels to you").

SEL (Opening Connection + Closing Reflection), ELL/SPED supports, and a Curated Resources block appear in every lesson.
