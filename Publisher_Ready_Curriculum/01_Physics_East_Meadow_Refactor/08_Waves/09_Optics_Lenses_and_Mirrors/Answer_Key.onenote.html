Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. 1/dᵢ = 1/f − 1/dₒ = 1/10 − 1/30 = 3/30 − 1/30 = 2/30 = 1/15 → dᵢ = 15 cm (real, inverted, since dₒ > f). · Rubric: correct equation AND 15 cm. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-6
  2. The magnifier’s enlarged image is virtual — the refracted rays only appear to come from it and never actually meet, so there is nothing to catch on a screen. · Rubric: identifies virtual + rays don’t meet. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-6
  3. Both converge light: a converging lens brings parallel rays to a focus by refraction, and a concave mirror brings them to a focus by reflection. Both form real, inverted images when the object is beyond f. · Rubric: both bring parallel rays to a focus. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-6

Exit Ticket Answer

  1. 1/dᵢ = 1/f − 1/dₒ = 1/12 − 1/36 = 3/36 − 1/36 = 2/36 = 1/18 → dᵢ = 18 cm.
  2. Real and inverted — the object (36 cm) is beyond the focal length (12 cm).
  3. No. Inside the focal length the image is virtual (upright, magnified); virtual images cannot be projected on a screen because the rays do not actually meet.

Tolerance/rubric: (a) 18 cm with the equation shown; (b) real AND inverted; (c) no + virtual reasoning. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS4-6.
