## Phenomenon

A pencil standing in a glass of water looks broken at the surface. A laser beam fired into a tank of water kinks the instant it hits the water. And a Pyrex stirrer placed in oil of just the right kind seems to vanish completely.

**Driving question:** What changes for the light right at the surface that makes it bend?
