## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. Snell's law: n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂ → sin θ₂ = (1.00 × sin 30°) / 1.50 = (1.00 × 0.500) / 1.50 = 0.333 → **θ₂ ≈ 19.5°.** · *Rubric:* correct setup AND ≈ 19–20°. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-6
2. Example: "Light bends toward the normal entering water **because** the water slows it down, **but** its frequency stays the same, **so** its wavelength shortens and the ray pivots toward the normal." · *Rubric:* Because/But/So with speed change as cause. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-6
3. Total internal reflection happens when light travels from a **slower (higher-n) medium toward a faster (lower-n) one** at an angle **greater than the critical angle** — the ray cannot refract out, so it reflects entirely. · *Rubric:* high-n → low-n AND past critical angle. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-6

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) sin θ₂ = (n₁ sin θ₁)/n₂ = (1.00 × sin 40°)/1.33 = (1.00 × 0.643)/1.33 = 0.483 → **θ₂ ≈ 28.9°** (about 29°).
(b) **Toward the normal**, because the light enters a slower, higher-index medium (water), so the refracted angle is smaller than the incident angle.
(c) The oil and Pyrex have the **same index of refraction**, so light does not change speed at the boundary — no refraction and no reflection — and the glass becomes invisible.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) 28°–29° with Snell's law shown; (b) toward the normal + slower-medium reason; (c) equal indices → no bend. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS4-6.
