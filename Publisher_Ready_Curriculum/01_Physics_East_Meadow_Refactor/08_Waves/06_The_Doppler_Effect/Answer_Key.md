## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. As the source approaches, each new wavefront is emitted a little closer to you, so the **wavelength is shorter**. By v = f·λ at constant speed, a shorter wavelength means a **higher frequency** → higher pitch. · *Rubric:* shorter wavelength → higher frequency. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
2. The star is moving **away** from us. Redshift means the wavelengths are stretched (longer/redder), which happens when the source recedes. · *Rubric:* away + redshift reasoning. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1 / HS-ESS1-2
3. **No.** The wave speed is set by the medium and stays the same. Only the observed wavelength and frequency change. · *Rubric:* speed unchanged + reason. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) **Higher.** Approaching, the wavefronts bunch up so the wavelength shortens; shorter wavelength → higher frequency → higher pitch.
(b) **Lower.** Receding, the wavefronts stretch so the wavelength lengthens → lower frequency → lower pitch.
(c) The galaxy is **moving away** from us. Because nearly all distant galaxies are redshifted, this is evidence that the **universe is expanding** (Big Bang / HS-ESS1-2).

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) higher + wavelength shortening; (b) lower + wavelength stretching; (c) receding AND expanding universe. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1, HS-ESS1-2.
