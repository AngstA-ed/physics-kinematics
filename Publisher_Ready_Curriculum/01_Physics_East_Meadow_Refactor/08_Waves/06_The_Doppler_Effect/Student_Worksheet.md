## Phenomenon

An ambulance races past you. The driver hears one steady siren the whole time — but you, on the curb, hear the pitch drop the instant it passes. Out in space, astronomers see the light from distant galaxies "shifted" toward red.

**Driving question:** If the siren makes the same pitch the whole time, why do *you* hear it change?

## Make It Make Sense

1. Why does an approaching source sound *higher* in pitch? Use the word *wavelength*.
2. A star's light is redshifted. Is it moving toward us or away from us? How do you know?
3. Does the Doppler effect change the *speed* of the wave in the medium? Explain.

## Exit Ticket

A race car drives past a spectator.

(a) Does the engine sound *higher* or *lower* as it approaches? Explain using wavelength.

(b) After it passes, what happens to the pitch and why?

(c) Astronomers see the light from a distant galaxy redshifted. What does this tell us about the galaxy's motion, and what does that suggest about the universe?
