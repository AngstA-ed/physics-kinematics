## Phenomenon

Your teacher plucks a thick low string and a thin high string the same way — they sound like completely different notes. The wave speed on each string is fixed by the string itself, yet the pitches are not the same.

**Driving question:** If two notes travel at the same speed, what must be different about their waves?

## Make It Make Sense

1. A wave travels at 340 m/s with a frequency of 170 Hz. Find its wavelength. Show v = f·λ rearranged.
2. At a fixed wave speed, you triple the frequency. What happens to the wavelength, and by what factor?
3. Write a Because/But/So sentence explaining why frequency and wavelength are inversely related at a constant wave speed.
