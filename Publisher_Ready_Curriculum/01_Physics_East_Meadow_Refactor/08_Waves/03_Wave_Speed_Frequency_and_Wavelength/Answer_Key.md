## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. Rearrange v = f·λ → **λ = v/f = 340 / 170 = 2 m.** · *Rubric:* correct rearrangement AND value with units. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
2. Tripling the frequency at fixed speed makes the wavelength **one-third** as long (inverse relationship). · *Rubric:* states 1/3 AND inverse reasoning. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
3. Example: "The wave speed stays constant **because** the medium sets how fast a disturbance moves, **but** the source can raise the frequency, **so** the wavelength must shrink to keep v = f·λ true." · *Rubric:* correct Because/But/So with inverse logic. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) **λ = v/f = 340 / 256 ≈ 1.33 m.**
(b) **Shorter — half the wavelength** (≈ 0.66 m), because doubling the frequency at the same speed halves the wavelength.
(c) **T = 1/f = 1/256 ≈ 0.0039 s** (about 3.9 ms).

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) 1.3–1.33 m with v = f·λ shown; (b) "half / shorter by factor 2"; (c) ≈ 0.0039 s. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1.
