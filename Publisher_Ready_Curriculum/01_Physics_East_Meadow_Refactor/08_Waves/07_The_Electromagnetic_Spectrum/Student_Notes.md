## Worked Example

**Problem.** An FM station broadcasts at 100 MHz (1.0 × 10⁸ Hz). Find its wavelength. (c = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s)

**Solution.**
1. Use v = f·λ with v = c: λ = c / f.
2. λ = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (1.0 × 10⁸) = __________ m.
3. Compare: an X-ray's wavelength is about 10⁻¹⁰ m — far __________ than a radio wave's. ✓
