## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. A **radio wave** has the longer wavelength. Radio has the lowest frequency; since v = f·λ with v = c fixed, the lowest frequency gives the longest wavelength. · *Rubric:* radio + v = f·λ reasoning. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
2. **No.** In a vacuum all EM waves travel at c = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s, so microwaves and visible light have the same speed. · *Rubric:* same speed + states c. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
3. λ = c/f = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (1.0 × 10⁸) = **3.0 m.** · *Rubric:* correct formula AND 3 m. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) Increasing frequency: **radio < infrared < visible < X-ray.**
(b) **Neither — they are equally fast.** Both travel at c = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s in a vacuum.
(c) UV has a **shorter wavelength** than visible light, because higher frequency means shorter wavelength (v = c fixed).

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) correct order; (b) equal speed = c; (c) UV shorter wavelength with reasoning. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1.
