Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. Amplitude tells you the energy. The larger the amplitude (height from rest to crest), the more energy the wave carries. Wavelength and frequency describe the wave’s shape and timing, not its energy. · Rubric: names amplitude AND links it to energy → full credit. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
  2. Period T = 1/f = 1/5 = 0.2 s per cycle. · Rubric: correct reciprocal formula AND correct value with units. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
  3. Wave A carries more energy because it has the larger amplitude. It does not travel faster — wave speed is set by the medium, not the amplitude, and both waves are in the same water. · Rubric: larger amplitude → more energy AND speed unchanged. · NYSSLS: HS-PS4-1
