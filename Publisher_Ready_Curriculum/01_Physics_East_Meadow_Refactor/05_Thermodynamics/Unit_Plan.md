## Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This unit develops the particle model of thermal energy and the conservation of energy as heat transfers between objects. It builds on the Energy unit (kinetic energy; energy is conserved and transfers) and culminates in a calorimetry investigation that satisfies the unit anchor, **HS-PS3-4**. Students move from *what temperature and thermal energy mean* (Lesson 01), to *which way heat flows and why energy spreads out* (Lesson 02), to *measuring and balancing that transfer quantitatively* (Lesson 03).

| # | Lesson | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Thermal Energy and Temperature | Temperature = average particle KE; thermal energy = total particle KE (depends on amount of substance) |
| 02 | Heat Transfer and the Second Law of Thermodynamics | Heat flows hot → cold to thermal equilibrium; conduction/convection/radiation; energy spreads to a more uniform distribution |
| 03 | Calorimetry — Thermal Tales | Q = m·c·ΔT; in a closed system heat lost = heat gained; specific heat |

## Pacing Calendar

| Day | Lesson | Phenomenon anchor | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01 Thermal Energy and Temperature | Sparkler vs. bathtub — which is "hotter," which holds more energy? | Restorative Circle (unit opener) |
| 2 | 02 Heat Transfer / Second Law | Penguin huddle; ice cube melting in a warm drink | Hochman |
| 3 | 03 Calorimetry — Thermal Tales | Mix hot + cold water; predict the final temperature | Active Learning (NYS Investigation) |

A double-period day on Day 3 lets the calorimetry investigation run long enough for multiple trials and the Q-balance analysis.

---

## Strategy Rotation

- **RESTORATIVE CIRCLE**: Lesson 01 — unit-opening circle ("a time you felt surprisingly hot or cold") that seeds the temperature-vs-thermal-energy distinction.
- **HOCHMAN** (sentence-level literacy): Lesson 02 — Because/But/So expansion on the direction of heat flow and the second law.
- **ACTIVE LEARNING** (NYS Investigation): Lesson 03 — students plan and conduct the Thermal Tales calorimetry lab and generate the heat lost = heat gained evidence.

SEL (Opening Connection + Closing Reflection), ELL/SPED supports, 2–4 figures, and a Curated Resources block appear in every lesson.
