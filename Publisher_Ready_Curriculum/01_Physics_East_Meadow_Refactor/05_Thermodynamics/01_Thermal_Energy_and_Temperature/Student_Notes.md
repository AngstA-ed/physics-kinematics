## Guided Notes

**Big idea:** Everything is made of particles that are always __________. The energy of that motion is __________ energy.

- **Thermal energy** is the __________ kinetic energy of __________ the particles.
- Thermal energy depends on two things: the __________ (average speed) **and** the __________ of substance (how many particles).

## Worked Example

**Problem.** A 0.25 kg cup of tea and a 500,000 kg swimming pool both read 25 °C. Which holds more thermal energy, and why?

**Solution.**
1. Same temperature → the particles move at the same __________ speed (same average kinetic energy each).
2. Thermal energy = average KE × the __________ of particles.
3. The pool has about __________ times as many particles → far more __________ kinetic energy. The **pool** holds vastly more thermal energy, even though it is not hotter.
