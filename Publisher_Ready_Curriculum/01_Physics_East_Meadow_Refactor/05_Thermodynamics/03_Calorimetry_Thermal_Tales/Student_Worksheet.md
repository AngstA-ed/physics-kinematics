## Phenomenon

Your teacher holds equal amounts of **hot water (80 °C)** and **cold water (20 °C)**. They are about to be poured together into an insulated cup. What will the thermometer read after they mix?

![Hot and cold samples meeting at one final temperature](figures/mixing_temps.png)

**Driving question:** When hot and cold water mix in a closed cup, what decides the final temperature?

**Reference Table formula:** Q = m·c·ΔT  (c_water = 4,186 J/kg·°C)
