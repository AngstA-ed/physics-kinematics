## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. Thermal energy flows from the **hotter** object to the **cooler** object. It never flows the other way on its own because that is the **second law of thermodynamics** — energy spontaneously spreads toward a more uniform distribution, not toward a more concentrated one. · *Rubric:* states hot → cold AND that the reverse is non-spontaneous / second law. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-4
2. **Conduction.** Fast-moving particles at the hot (bowl) end collide with neighboring particles, passing energy along the metal until the handle warms. · *Rubric:* names conduction AND mentions particle-to-particle transfer. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-4
3. Rotating moves cold edge birds to the warm center and warm center birds to the edge, so thermal energy **spreads more uniformly** through the whole group instead of staying concentrated — keeping every penguin warm enough to survive. · *Rubric:* links rotation to a more uniform energy distribution. · NYSSLS: HS-PS3-4

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) Thermal energy flows from the **warm spoon to the cold water** — heat always flows from hotter to cooler.
(b) The final situation is **thermal equilibrium**: the spoon and the water reach the **same** temperature, and there is no further net flow of energy between them.
(c) The handle warms by **conduction**. Second law (student wording): *in a closed system, thermal energy spreads out on its own to a more uniform distribution* (energy goes from concentrated/hot to spread-out, never the reverse spontaneously).

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) hot → cold (spoon → water); (b) "thermal equilibrium" AND "same temperature / no net flow"; (c) conduction AND a correct paraphrase of the second law. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS3-4.
