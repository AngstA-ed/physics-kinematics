## Guided Notes

**Golden rule of rearranging:** whatever I do to one side of an equation, I do to the __________ side. I undo an operation with its __________ (multiply undoes divide; subtract undoes add).

Starting from **v = d / t**:

- To isolate **d**: *d* is being __________ by *t*, so __________ both sides by *t* → **d = __________**.
- To isolate **t**: get *t* out of the bottom, then __________ → **t = __________**.

## Worked Example

**Problem.** A zipline cable is 26 m long (the hypotenuse) and leaves the top at 18° below horizontal. Find the tower height (the rise).

**Solution.**
1. The height is the side __________ the 18° angle, and I was given the __________.
2. Opposite over hypotenuse means I use __________: sin 18° = height / 26.
3. Solve: height = 26 × sin 18° = 26 × 0.309 ≈ __________ m.
4. (Run = 26 × cos 18° ≈ 24.7 m — the adjacent side.)
