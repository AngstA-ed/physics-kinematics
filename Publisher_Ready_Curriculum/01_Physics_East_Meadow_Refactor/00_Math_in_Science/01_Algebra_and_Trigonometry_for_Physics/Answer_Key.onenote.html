Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. Start from a = (v − u) / t. Multiply both sides by t: a t = v − u. Add u to both sides: v = u + a t. · Rubric: multiplies by t AND adds u → full credit; stopping at a t = v − u earns partial. · Practice: Using Mathematics and Computational Thinking.
  2. The rise is the side opposite the 10° angle (it is across from the angle, not touching it along the ground). · Rubric: “opposite” with a one-phrase justification. · CCC: Scale, Proportion, and Quantity.
  3. Opposite over hypotenuse → use sine: rise = 5.0 × sin 10° = 5.0 × 0.1736 ≈ 0.87 m. · Rubric: correct ratio (sine) AND answer 0.85–0.88 m with units. Accept 0.9 m if rounding is shown. · Common error: using cosine (gives 4.9 m) — that is the ground run, not the rise.

Exit Ticket Answer

  1. t = d / v. From v = d / t, multiply both sides by t to get v t = d, then divide both sides by v.
  2. The rise is the side opposite the 10° angle.
  3. sin 10° = rise / 5.0, so rise = 5.0 × sin 10° ≈ 0.87 m. (Calculator in degree mode: sin 10° ≈ 0.174.)

Tolerance/rubric: (a) must show t = d / v; (b) “opposite”; (c) names sine AND gives 0.85–0.88 m with units. 1 point each. The most common error is radian mode — an answer near 0.43 m signals it. Practice: Using Mathematics and Computational Thinking; CCC: Scale, Proportion, and Quantity.
