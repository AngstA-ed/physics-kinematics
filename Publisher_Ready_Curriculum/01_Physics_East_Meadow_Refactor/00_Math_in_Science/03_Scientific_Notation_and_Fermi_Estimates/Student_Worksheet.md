## Phenomenon

In "Scale of the Universe" you zoom from an atom (about 10⁻¹⁰ m) all the way out to a galaxy (about 10²¹ m) — dozens of powers of ten. Then your teacher asks a number nobody could ever count: **how many basketballs would fill this gym?**

**Driving question:** How do scientists write — and estimate — numbers far too big or too small to count?
