## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. 920,000 = **9.2 × 10⁵**; 0.00045 = **4.5 × 10⁻⁴**. · *Rubric:* leading number 1–10 AND correct exponent (sign + size). Reject 92 × 10⁴ or 0.45 × 10⁻³. · Practice: Using Mathematics & Computational Thinking.
2. **3 × 10⁵ is larger.** Compare exponents first: 5 beats 4, a whole order of magnitude, so the leading numbers don't matter. · *Rubric:* correct choice AND "bigger exponent" reasoning. · CCC: Scale, Proportion, and Quantity.
3. From 10⁻⁶ to 10⁰ is **6 orders of magnitude** (count the exponent steps: −6 up to 0). · *Rubric:* 6, by subtracting exponents. · Common error: answering "1,000,000" — that's the *factor*, the question asks for the number of orders.

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) 84,000 = **8.4 × 10⁴**; 0.00072 = **7.2 × 10⁻⁴**.
(b) **4 × 10⁷ is larger** — its exponent (7) is bigger than 6, one full order of magnitude up, so it outweighs the larger leading digit of 9 × 10⁶.
(c) **~10⁴ grains.** Sample reasoning: a mug ≈ 300 mL and a grain of rice ≈ 0.02 mL, so 300 ÷ 0.02 ≈ 15,000 ≈ 10⁴. Any answer of 10³–10⁵ with reasoning is full credit.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) both correct, leading number 1–10; (b) 4 × 10⁷ with exponent reasoning; (c) a single power of ten in the 10³–10⁵ range with one sentence of round-number reasoning. 1 point each. Practice: Using Mathematics & Computational Thinking; CCC: Scale, Proportion, and Quantity.
