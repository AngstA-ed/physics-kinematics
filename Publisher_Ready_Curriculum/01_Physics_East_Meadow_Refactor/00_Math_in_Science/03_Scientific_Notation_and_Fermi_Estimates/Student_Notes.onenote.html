Guided Notes

The form: scientific notation is (a number from 1 to 10) × 10^(exponent). The leading number must be at least 1 and less than __________.

Moving the decimal:

  • Big numbers → exponent is __________ (positive). 47,000 = 4.7 × 10^___ (decimal moved __________ places left).
  • Small numbers → exponent is __________ (negative). 0.006 = 6 × 10^___ (decimal moved __________ places right).

Comparing sizes: the number with the larger __________ is bigger. Each step in the exponent is a factor of __________. So 10⁶ is __________ times bigger than 10³.

Order of magnitude = the power of ten a quantity is nearest. It answers “what level of size?” not “exactly how many?”
