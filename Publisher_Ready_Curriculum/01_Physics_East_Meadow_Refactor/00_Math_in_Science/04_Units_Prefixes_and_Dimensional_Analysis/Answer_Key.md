## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. 5,000 m = **5 km**. The number got **smaller** because a kilometer is a bigger unit, so it takes fewer of them for the same length. · *Rubric:* 5 km AND the "bigger unit → fewer of them" reasoning. · Practice: Using Mathematics & Computational Thinking.
2. 3.2 m = **320 cm** (centi = 10⁻² m, so 1 m = 100 cm; move the decimal two places right). · *Rubric:* 320 cm with units. · CCC: Scale, Proportion, and Quantity.

The answer came out as km·m/h because a conversion factor was written **upside down** — the km did not cancel. **Fix:** write the factor as (1000 m / 1 km) so km sits on the bottom and cancels, and (1 h / 3600 s) so h cancels. · *Rubric:* identifies the inverted factor AND states the correct orientation. · Common error: blaming the arithmetic instead of the unit orientation.

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) 2.5 km = 2.5 × 1000 = **2,500 m**.
(b) 45 mm = 45 × 10⁻³ = **0.045 m** (milli = 10⁻³; move the decimal three places left).
(c) 72 km/h × (1000 m / 1 km) × (1 h / 3600 s) = (72 × 1000) ÷ 3600 = **20 m/s**. The **km** cancels with the km on the bottom of the first ratio, and the **h** cancels with the h on the top of the second ratio, leaving m/s.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) 2,500 m; (b) 0.045 m (accept 4.5 × 10⁻² m); (c) 20 m/s AND km + h circled/identified as the canceling units. 1 point each. Practice: Using Mathematics & Computational Thinking; CCC: Scale, Proportion, and Quantity.
