## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. **Time is independent** (x-axis) — it ticks on its own and we choose when to read it; **distance is dependent** (y-axis) — it responds to how far the walker has gone. · *Rubric:* both roles correct AND on the correct axes. · Practice: Analyzing & Interpreting Data.
2. slope = rise / run = 6 / 3 = **2**. · *Rubric:* 2, shown as rise over run. · CCC: Patterns.
3. The steeper line (A) means y changes **faster** — a larger increase in y for each step in x (a greater rate of change). · *Rubric:* connects steepness to rate of change, not to the size of a single value. · Common error: "A has bigger numbers" — steepness is about the *rate*, not the height.

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) **Mass is the independent variable** (x-axis); **length is the dependent variable** (y-axis).
(b) slope = rise / run = 8 cm / 200 g = **0.04 cm/g**.
(c) The spring stretches **0.04 cm for every extra gram** hung on it — that is its stretch rate. (The 12 cm start is its unstretched length, the y-intercept.)

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) both roles + correct axes; (b) 0.04 cm/g with units (accept 0.04 cm per gram); (c) "0.04 cm per gram of stretch" in plain words. 1 point each. Practice: Using Mathematics & Computational Thinking; CCC: Patterns.
