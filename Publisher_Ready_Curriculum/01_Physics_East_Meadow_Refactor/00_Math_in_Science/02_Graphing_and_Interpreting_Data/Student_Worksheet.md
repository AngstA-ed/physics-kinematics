## Exit Ticket

A class plots a stretched spring's length (cm) versus the mass hung on it (g). The line is straight and starts at 12 cm when 0 g is hung.

(a) Which is the independent variable and which is the dependent variable?

(b) The line rises 8 cm over a run of 200 g. What is the slope, including units?

(c) Explain in plain words what that slope tells you about the spring.
