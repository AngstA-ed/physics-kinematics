Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This unit develops momentum as a conserved quantity and impulse as the agent that changes it. It builds directly on Forces (Newton’s three laws, net force, inertia) and on Kinematics (velocity as a vector), and it sets up Energy (kinetic energy bookkeeping in collisions). The arc moves from a single object’s momentum, to the impulse that changes it, to the conservation of total momentum in a system, and finally to classifying collisions by whether kinetic energy is conserved.

# Lesson Core idea Reference-table formula
01 Momentum Momentum p = m·v is a vector; more mass or more speed → more momentum p = m·v
02 Impulse and the Impulse–Momentum Theorem Impulse J = F·Δt = Δp; extending contact time reduces force J = F·Δt = Δp
03 Conservation of Momentum Total momentum is conserved when external net force = 0; before = after Σp_before = Σp_after
04 Collisions: Elastic and Inelastic Momentum conserved in both; KE conserved only in elastic KE = ½mv²

Pacing Calendar

Double-period days (every other day) allow the investigation phases of Lessons 03–04 to run long with a cart-collision lab and a PhET station rotation.

Strategy Rotation

  • RESTORATIVE CIRCLE: Lesson 01 — unit-opening circle (“something hard to stop once it got going”).
  • HOCHMAN (literacy): Lesson 02 — Because/But/So expansion and an appositive on J = F·Δt = Δp (why airbags reduce force).
  • ACTIVE LEARNING: Lesson 03 — predict-measure-reconcile with carts and a shared class data table; PhET total-momentum readout.
  • BTC (Building Thinking Classrooms): Lesson 04 — visibly random groups + vertical non-permanent surfaces on a momentum-then-energy thin-slice.

SEL (Opening Connection + Closing Reflection), ELL/SPED supports, and a Curated Resources block appear in every lesson.
