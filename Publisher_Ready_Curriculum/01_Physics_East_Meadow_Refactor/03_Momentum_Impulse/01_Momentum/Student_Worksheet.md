## Make It Make Sense

1. A 1,200 kg car moves East at 20 m/s. Calculate its momentum, with units and direction.
2. Two carts roll at the same speed, but Cart B has twice the mass of Cart A. How do their momenta compare?
3. A golf ball and a baseball are thrown so they have the *same momentum*. The golf ball is lighter. What must be true about its speed? Explain.
