## Worked Example

**Problem.** A 1,200 kg car moves East at 20 m/s. Find its momentum.

**Solution.**
1. Write the formula: p = m · ____ .
2. Substitute: p = (1,200 kg)(____ m/s).
3. Compute: p = __________ kg·m/s.
4. Direction: __________ (same as the velocity).
