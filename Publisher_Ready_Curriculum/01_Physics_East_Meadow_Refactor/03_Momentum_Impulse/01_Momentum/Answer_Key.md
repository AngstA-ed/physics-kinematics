## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. p = m·v = (1,200 kg)(20 m/s) = **24,000 kg·m/s, East**. · *Rubric:* correct product AND direction (East). · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-2
2. Cart B has **twice the momentum** of Cart A. Same velocity, so momentum is proportional to mass; double the mass → double the momentum. · *Rubric:* states 2× AND links to p = m·v with equal v. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-2
3. The golf ball must be moving **faster**. Since p = m·v is the same for both and the golf ball has less mass, its velocity must be larger to make up the difference. · *Rubric:* "faster" AND reasons from m·v held constant. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-2

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) p = m·v = (0.145 kg)(40 m/s) = **5.8 kg·m/s, West**.
(b) Golf ball: p = (0.045 kg)(70 m/s) = **3.15 kg·m/s**. Since 5.8 > 3.15, the **baseball has more momentum**.
(c) **Vector** — momentum has a direction (the same direction as the velocity), not just a size.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) 5.8 kg·m/s with unit AND direction West; (b) golf ball 3.15 kg·m/s and correct comparison; (c) "vector" + a direction-based reason. 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS2-2.
