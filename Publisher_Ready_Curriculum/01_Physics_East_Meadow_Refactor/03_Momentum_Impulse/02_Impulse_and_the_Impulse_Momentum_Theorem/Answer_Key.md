## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. The pillow makes the **contact time (Δt) bigger**. Since J = F·Δt and the impulse (Δp) is fixed, a larger Δt means a **smaller force** on the egg, so it does not break. · *Rubric:* identifies Δt increase AND force decrease for fixed impulse. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-3
2. The crumple zone **extends the collision time**. The change in momentum is set by the crash, so by spreading it over more time the average force on the passengers drops (F = Δp/Δt) — that is what keeps the people safe even though the car is wrecked. · *Rubric:* links longer Δt → smaller force using J = F·Δt. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-3
3. J = Δp = m·Δv = (0.5 kg)(8 m/s) = **4 kg·m/s (4 N·s)**, directed opposite the ball's motion. · *Rubric:* correct magnitude 4 N·s with unit. · NYSSLS: HS-PS2-2

## Exit Ticket Answer

(a) J = Δp = m·Δv = (0.5 kg)(0 − 8 m/s) = **−4 kg·m/s**, i.e. **4 N·s opposite the motion**.
(b) Glove: F = Δp/Δt = 4 / 0.4 = **10 N**. Bare hand: F = 4 / 0.05 = **80 N**.
(c) The **impulse is the same**, but the glove spreads it over a **longer time**, so by F = Δp/Δt the **force is smaller** — that is why it hurts less.

*Tolerance/rubric:* (a) 4 N·s with unit AND direction; (b) 10 N and 80 N (allow rounding); (c) "same impulse, longer time, smaller force." 1 point each. NYSSLS: HS-PS2-3.
