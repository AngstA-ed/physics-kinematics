Worked Example

Problem. A 0.5 kg ball moving at 8 m/s is caught and stopped. Compare the force for a glove (Δt = 0.4 s) and a bare hand (Δt = 0.05 s).

Solution. 1. Impulse needed: J = Δp = m·Δv = (0.5)(0 − 8) = __________ kg·m/s (magnitude 4 N·s). 2. Glove: F = Δp / Δt = 4 / 0.4 = __________ N. 3. Bare hand: F = Δp / Δt = 4 / 0.05 = __________ N. 4. Same impulse, but the glove uses __________ time, so the force is __________.
