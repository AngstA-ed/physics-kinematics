Worked Example

Problem. A 2 kg cart moving East at 3 m/s collides with a 1 kg cart at rest and they stick together. Find their final velocity.

Solution. 1. Total before: Σp = (2)(3) + (1)(0) = __________ kg·m/s East. 2. After sticking, combined mass = __________ kg. 3. Conservation: Σp_before = Σp_after → 6 = (3)(v). 4. Solve: v = __________ m/s, directed __________.
