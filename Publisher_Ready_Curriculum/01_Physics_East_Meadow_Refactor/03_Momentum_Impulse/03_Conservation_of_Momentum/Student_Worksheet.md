## Phenomenon

Two ice skaters stand still, facing each other, then push off. Both glide away in opposite directions. On a cart track, a moving cart hits a still cart and the motion transfers.

**Driving question:** When two objects interact, what happens to the *total* momentum of the system?

## Make It Make Sense

1. Two skaters at rest push apart. The total before is zero. Explain why the total after is also zero, even though both are now moving.
2. A 2 kg cart moving at 3 m/s East hits a still 1 kg cart and they stick. What outside force, if present, would stop momentum from being conserved?
3. If two skaters of different mass push apart, which one moves faster? Justify with p = m·v and the idea that the total stays zero.
