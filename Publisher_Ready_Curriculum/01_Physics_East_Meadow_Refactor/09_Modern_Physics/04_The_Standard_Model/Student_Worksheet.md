## Phenomenon

Zoom in on a chair: it's made of atoms. An atom is a nucleus plus electrons. The nucleus is protons and neutrons. So what is a *proton* made of? Scientists kept zooming — and found that the zoom finally **stops** at a small set of particles that everything else is built from.

**Driving question:** What are the smallest building blocks of matter, and how do scientists organize them?
