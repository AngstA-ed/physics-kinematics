Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This closing unit reframes everything students have learned about waves, energy, and matter at the scale of the atom and the nucleus. Light, which earlier behaved as a wave, now also behaves as a stream of energy packets called photons. Matter, which earlier was modeled as solid particles, is now organized into a small set of fundamental particles. The unit develops and uses models (a core SEP) as the through-line: each lesson revises a model when new evidence demands it. It builds on Waves (frequency and wavelength) and Energy, and it connects outward to chemistry (nuclear change) and astronomy (starlight and the Big Bang).

# Lesson Core idea
01 The Photoelectric Effect Light is quantized: photons of energy E = h·f eject electrons only above a threshold frequency
02 Models of the Atom The atomic model evolved (Dalton → Thomson → Rutherford → Bohr → quantum) as evidence accumulated
03 Energy-Level Diagrams and Spectra Electrons occupy discrete energy levels; photon jumps produce line spectra that fingerprint elements
04 The Standard Model Matter is built from quarks and leptons; forces are carried by bosons
05 Fusion, Fission, and Radioactive Decay Nuclear changes release enormous energy (E = mc², scale only); they power stars and form elements

Double-period days (every other day) let the Explore phases of Lessons 01 and 03 run long with the PhET and ophysics simulations as full labs.

Strategy Rotation

  • HOCHMAN (literacy): Lessons 01, 04 — Because/But/So expansion and appositives on the key relationships (E = h·f and threshold frequency; quarks/leptons/bosons as an organizing model).
  • ACTIVE LEARNING: Lessons 02, 05 — gold-foil claim-evidence card sort and model-revision gallery; the fission/fusion/decay station rotation.
  • BTC (Building Thinking Classrooms): Lesson 03 — random groups + vertical non-permanent surfaces on an energy-level/spectrum thin-slice.

SEL (Opening Connection + Closing Reflection), ELL/SPED supports, and a Curated Resources block appear in every lesson.
