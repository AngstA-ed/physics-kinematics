## Phenomenon

A campfire burns out overnight. The **Sun** has blazed for about five billion years — and is barely halfway done. No chemical fire could last that long. The same word, *nuclear*, shows up in the Sun, in power plants, and in carbon-14 dating of ancient bones.

**Driving question:** Where does nuclear energy come from, why is it so enormous, and how does the nucleus change in each case?

## Make It Make Sense

2. The Sun combines hydrogen nuclei into helium. Which process is this, and what does it have to do with how stars and elements form?
