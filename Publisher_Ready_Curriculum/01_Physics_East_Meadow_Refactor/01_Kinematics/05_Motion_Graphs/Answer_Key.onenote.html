Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. Watch the dot move with constant velocity 4 m/s for 5 seconds. Sketch its x-t graph and its v-t graph. Expected: x-t: straight line from (0, 0) to (5, 20), slope = +4 (constant velocity). v-t: horizontal line at v = 4 m/s for 0–5 s. Rubric: Full credit if both graphs have correct shape and at least one labeled value (slope or height). Half credit for one graph. NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1.

  2. An object’s v-t graph rises from 0 at t = 0 to 10 m/s at t = 5 s. What is the acceleration? What is the displacement during this interval? Expected: Acceleration = (10 − 0) / 5 = 2 m/s². Displacement = area under the v-t graph = ½ · base · height = ½ · 5 · 10 = 25 m. Rubric: Full credit if both numbers correct with units and student names “area under v-t” reasoning. Half credit for either alone. NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1.

  3. The x-t graph of an object is a horizontal line. What is the velocity? Sketch the corresponding v-t graph. Expected: Horizontal x-t means position is unchanging, so velocity = 0. v-t graph is a horizontal line at v = 0 (i.e., the time axis itself). Rubric: Full credit if “velocity = 0” stated AND v-t sketched as horizontal at zero. Common error: students sketch v-t at the same height as x-t — coach: “It’s the slope of x-t that’s velocity, not the height.” NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1.

Exit Ticket Answer

v-t graph as described:

  1. Accelerations:
  • 0–4 s: slope = (8 − 0)/4 = +2 m/s² (speeding up).
  • 4–7 s: slope = 0 → 0 m/s² (constant velocity).
  • 7–10 s: slope = (0 − 8)/3 ≈ −2.67 m/s² (slowing down).

  1. Qualitative x-t shape: a curve that gets steeper from 0 to 4 s (parabolic, opening up), then a straight line (constant slope) from 4 to 7 s, then a curve that flattens from 7 to 10 s (deceleration). Position is monotonically increasing throughout.

  2. Total displacement = area under v-t graph = (½ · 4 · 8) + (3 · 8) + (½ · 3 · 8) = 16 + 24 + 12 = 52 m.

Rubric: (a) 1 pt for each of three values (3 pts). (b) 1 pt for monotonic-increasing shape with correct concavity changes. (c) 2 pts: 1 for correct area calculation, 1 for “52 m” with units. Tolerance: ±0.5 on parts (a) and (c).

NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1.

Closing Reflection (rubric)

Closing reflection is formative, not graded. Award full credit for any honest response. Look for:

  • Surprise / interest signal in answer 1 (e.g., “I didn’t realize the area under v-t was the displacement”)
  • Naming a peer or self-acknowledgment in answer 2
