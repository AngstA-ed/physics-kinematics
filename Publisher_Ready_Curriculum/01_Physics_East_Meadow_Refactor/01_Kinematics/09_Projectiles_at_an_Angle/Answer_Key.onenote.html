Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

  1. Set v₀ = 12 m/s and θ = 30°. What is the range? What is the time-of-flight? Expected: Range = v₀² sin(2θ)/g = 144 · sin(60°)/9.8 = 144 · 0.866/9.8 ≈ 12.7 m. Time-of-flight = 2·v₀·sin(θ)/g = 2 · 12 · 0.5/9.8 ≈ 1.22 s. Rubric: Full credit if both answers correct with units. Half credit for one. Tolerance: ±0.3 m, ±0.05 s. NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1.

  2. At the same v₀ = 12 m/s, set θ = 60°. Compare its range and time-of-flight to the 30° launch. Expected: Range at 60° = 144 · sin(120°)/9.8 = 144 · 0.866/9.8 ≈ 12.7 m — same as 30° (symmetry around 45°). Time-of-flight at 60° = 2 · 12 · sin(60°)/9.8 = 2 · 12 · 0.866/9.8 ≈ 2.12 s — much longer. The 60° kick stays in the air longer but lands at the same horizontal distance. Rubric: Full credit if (a) student notes “same range” AND (b) longer time-of-flight at 60° with the values. Half credit for one observation. Insight credit for naming “symmetric around 45°.” NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1 (patterns).

  3. At what launch angle is the range maximized? What is the maximum range at v₀ = 12 m/s? Expected: Range is maximized at θ = 45°. Max range = v₀²/g = 144/9.8 ≈ 14.7 m (since sin(90°) = 1). Rubric: Full credit for “45°” + max range value. Half credit for “45°” without value. Bonus credit for showing sin(2θ) = sin(90°) = 1 reasoning. NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1.

Exit Ticket Answer

Soccer kick: v₀ = 14 m/s at θ = 35°. g = 9.8 m/s².

  1. Components: v_x = v₀ cos(35°) = 14 × 0.819 ≈ 11.47 m/s. v_y = v₀ sin(35°) = 14 × 0.574 ≈ 8.03 m/s.
  2. Time-of-flight = 2·v_y/g = 2 × 8.03/9.8 ≈ 1.64 s.
  3. Range = v_x · t_of = 11.47 × 1.64 ≈ 18.8 m. (Or directly: R = v₀²·sin(2θ)/g = 196·sin(70°)/9.8 ≈ 18.8 m.)
  4. Comparison with 55°: by symmetry around 45°, 35° and 55° are symmetric (35° = 45° − 10°; 55° = 45° + 10°). The range is the same: ≈ 18.8 m. The 55° kick has a longer time-of-flight but the same range.

Rubric: (a) 1 pt; (b) 1 pt; (c) 1 pt; (d) 1 pt for “same range” with the symmetry reasoning. Tolerance: ±0.3 m on range, ±0.05 s on time, ±0.2 m/s on components.

NYSSLS: HS-PS2-1.
