The NYSSLS thread for the unit is HS-PS2-1: Newton’s Second Law of Motion — Analyze data to support the claim that Newton’s second law of motion describes the mathematical relationship among the net force on a macroscopic object, its mass, and its acceleration. (Cause and Effect)

Vectors (Lesson 01) and Acceleration (Lesson 04) are the explicitly NYSSLS-tagged rows in the source MD. The remaining lessons carry the thread by establishing the vector quantities (displacement, velocity, acceleration components) that HS-PS2-1 requires students to reason about. Conservation of momentum (HS-PS2-2) and gravitational/electrostatic forces (HS-PS2-4) belong to the Forces unit that follows.

  • Hochman writing — 1–2× per week
  • Active learning — 1–2× per week

  • Restorative opening circle — where appropriate (canonical placements: unit opener; day after a high-stakes assessment)

The unit is single-PE focused: every lesson reinforces HS-PS2-1. The remaining HS-PS2-* PEs (momentum, collision-design, gravity/Coulomb, magnetic-electric fields) live in the Forces, Momentum, and Electrostatics units.

Cumulative vocabulary review (recommended for last 5 minutes of Lesson 09 or the assessment day): a 27-card matching deck where students match each term to its definition. Use as a Hochman kernel sentence prompt: “The ___ of a projectile is ___.” for any term.
