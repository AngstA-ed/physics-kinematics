Unit: Kinematics — Lesson 01: Vectors

RESTORATIVE CIRCLE ACTIVE LEARNING

Phenomenon

Watch this short animation: arrows from Physics Classroom — Vector Animations show two arrows being added head to tail. Notice that the order in which you add them does not change where the second arrow ends up.

Driving Question

How can we add two quantities that have direction, not just size?

Notice & Wonder

List two things you notice and two things you wonder.

Turn to your partner. Share one thing you noticed about the arrows and one thing you wondered. Pick a wondering you would like the class to figure out today.

Initial Model

Sketch your first explanation. If arrow A points 4 squares right and arrow B points 3 squares up, where does the second arrow's tip end up — and how would you describe that ending point in words?

Investigate

Drag the sliders to change the magnitudes of vector A (right) and vector B (up). Watch how the dashed resultant arrow R changes. Predict before you slide.

Storyboard frame 1. Vector A points right, magnitude 4 units. Vector B points up, magnitude 3 units, drawn head-to-tail from the tip of A.

Storyboard frame 2. The resultant R is the dashed arrow from the tail of A to the head of B. Its magnitude is √(4² + 3²) = 5 units. R points about 37° above the +x axis.

Storyboard frame 3. If B grew to 6 units, R would be √(16 + 36) ≈ 7.2 units — the resultant grows when either input vector grows. Direction also shifts steeper (about 56° above +x).

If A points right (magnitude 4) and B points up (magnitude 3), the resultant comes out to 5 — not 7. Use the grid to convince your partner why.

Make It Make Sense

  1. Slide the controls so A = 3 and B = 4. What is the magnitude and direction of the resultant?
  2. Describe what happens to the resultant when A and B point in the same direction. What about opposite directions?
  3. If a vector has magnitude 10 and points 30° above the x-axis, what are its x- and y-components?
Stuck? Sentence frame

"The resultant of A + B has magnitude ___ because ___."

Key Vocabulary (max 3)

  • vector — a quantity with both magnitude and direction
  • magnitude — the size or length of a vector
  • direction — the way a vector points, often given as an angle

Revise Your Model

Update your sketch from the Initial Model. Use the words magnitude and direction in one sentence to describe where the second arrow ends up.

Return to the Phenomenon

Open the original Physics Classroom animation again. Predict the resultant of the two arrows shown using today's vocabulary. Was your prediction in the Initial Model close? What changed?

Exit Ticket + Closing Reflection

  1. A boat sails 5 km east, then 12 km north. Sketch the resultant displacement on a grid and find its magnitude. Show one sentence of reasoning.
