## Make-It-Make-Sense Answers

1. **Slide a = +3 m/s² and watch the dot for 4 s. What is the dot's velocity at the end? How far did it travel from rest?**
   *Expected:* v = a·t = 3 × 4 = **12 m/s**. Distance from rest with constant a: d = ½ · a · t² = ½ · 3 · 16 = **24 m**.
   *Rubric:* Full credit if both answers correct with units. Half credit if velocity correct but distance not. Tolerance: ±0.
   *NYSSLS:* HS-PS2-1 (kinematic relationships).

2. **What does the v-t graph slope tell you? Sketch a v-t graph for a car that speeds up at 2 m/s² for 5 s, then holds steady for 3 s.**
   *Expected:* Slope of v-t = **acceleration**. Sketch: a straight line from v=0 at t=0 to v=10 m/s at t=5 s (slope +2 m/s²), then a horizontal line at v=10 m/s from t=5 to t=8 s (slope 0).
   *Rubric:* Full credit if (a) student names "slope = acceleration" AND (b) sketch shows the two segments with correct slopes and the right values at the joint. Half credit for sketch alone or naming alone.
   *NYSSLS:* HS-PS2-1.

3. **A car moving west has a negative acceleration (in the eastward-positive convention). Is the car speeding up or slowing down? Explain.**
   *Expected:* The car is **speeding up**. The car's velocity is negative (westward, in this convention). A negative acceleration means the acceleration vector also points west — same direction as the velocity. Same-direction acceleration speeds the object up. The sign of acceleration alone does not say "slowing down" — you must compare it to the direction of motion.
   *Rubric:* Full credit for "speeding up" plus the comparison reasoning. Half credit for "speeding up" without justification. *No credit* for "slowing down" — this catches the dominant misconception.
   *NYSSLS:* HS-PS2-1.

## Exit Ticket Answer

A sprinter starts from rest and reaches 9 m/s in 3 seconds.

(a) Average acceleration = (9 − 0) / 3 = **3 m/s² (forward)**.
(b) v-t sketch: straight line from (t=0, v=0) to (t=3, v=9). Slope = +3.
(c) Slow-down phase: a = (0 − 9) / 6 = **−1.5 m/s²**. **Negative** in the forward-positive convention because the velocity *decreased*, so the acceleration vector points opposite the motion. The runner is slowing down (deceleration).

*Rubric:* (a) 1 pt; (b) 1 pt for correct slope and direction; (c) 2 pts: 1 for correct number, 1 for naming "negative because the velocity is decreasing / acceleration opposes motion." Tolerance: ±0.

*NYSSLS:* HS-PS2-1.
