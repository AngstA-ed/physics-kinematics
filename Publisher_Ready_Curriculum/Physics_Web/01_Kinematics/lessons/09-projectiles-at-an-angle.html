Lesson 09 · Unit: Kinematics

Projectiles at an Angle

What launch angle produces the maximum horizontal range — and why?

Active Learning
Download teacher guide (DOCX)

Phenomenon

Watch Jamaal Murray catches his own hail mary. The player tosses the ball with one hand, sprints around the defender, and catches the same ball before it hits the ground. He has internalized — without equations — the launch angle and air-time that bring the ball back to him.

Driving Question

What launch angle produces the maximum horizontal range — and why?

Notice & Wonder

If you had to throw a softball as far as possible (same speed every time), what angle would you choose? Why?

Initial Model

Sketch trajectories for three launches at the same v₀: 20°, 45°, and 70°. Predict which lands farthest.

Investigate

Slide v₀ and θ. Press Launch. The trajectory traces in real time, and the readouts show range and time-of-flight. Toggle show optimal angle to highlight the 45° prediction.

v_x = — m/s · v_y = — m/s

Range: — m · time-of-flight: — s · max height: — m

Try θ = 30° and θ = 60° at the same v₀. The ranges are the same! Why? Use today's vocabulary in your answer.

Make It Make Sense

  1. Set v₀ = 12 m/s and θ = 30°. What is the range? What is the time-of-flight?
  2. At the same v₀ = 12 m/s, set θ = 60°. Compare its range and time-of-flight to the 30° launch.
  3. At what launch angle is the range maximized? What is the maximum range at v₀ = 12 m/s?
Stuck? Sentence frame

"At a launch angle of ___°, the range is ___ m because v_x = ___ and v_y = ___."

Key Vocabulary (max 3)

  • launch angle (θ) — the angle above horizontal at which the projectile is launched
  • range equation — R = v₀² sin(2θ) / g, valid for level ground and no air resistance
  • optimal angle — 45° on level ground (in vacuum); the angle that produces maximum range

Revise Your Model

Update your initial trajectory sketches. Add the *range* and *time-of-flight* values for each angle. Highlight the symmetric pairs (15°/75°, 30°/60°).

Return to the Phenomenon

Use today's tools to estimate Jamaal Murray's hail mary throw. If the ball was airborne for ~2.5 s and traveled ~25 m horizontally, what was the launch angle and launch speed (approximately)?

Exit Ticket + Closing Reflection

  1. A soccer player kicks the ball at 14 m/s at 35° above horizontal. (a) v_x and v_y components? (b) Time-of-flight? (c) Range? (d) Predict whether kicking at 55° (same speed) lands closer or farther. Explain.
  2. Reflection: What is one thing that surprised you today? Who helped you make sense of something?
