## Phenomenon

A green plant sits on a windowsill, motionless, for weeks. It is not plugged into anything — just air, water, and light. Yet it grows new leaves out of almost nothing. If you dried one of those leaves and set it on fire, it would release heat and light. That heat had to come from somewhere. The plant "charged up" the leaf with energy — and burning it pays the energy back out.

**Driving question:** How does a plant "capture" sunlight as chemical energy you can later eat?
