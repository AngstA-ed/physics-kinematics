## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Energy and Matter (flows and conservation):** During photosynthesis, ______________ energy from the Sun flows into a plant and is stored as ______________ energy in the bonds of sugar. The atoms are not created or destroyed — they are only ______________. Later, when the sugar is burned or eaten, that stored energy is ______________ (not created), so energy is ______________.

**The photosynthesis equation:**

$$6\ \text{CO}_2 + 6\ \text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow{\text{light}} \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\ \text{O}_2$$

- **Inputs (reactants):** ______________ and ______________ — these store ______________ (low / high) energy.
- **Outputs (products):** ______________ and ______________ — these store ______________ (low / high) energy.
- **Energy source that powers the reaction:** ______________

**The atoms balance (matter is conserved):**

| Element | Left (reactants) | Right (products) |
|---|---|---|
| C | 6 | 6 |
| H | 12 | 12 |
| O | 18 | 18 |

Because every atom on the left reappears on the right, no atoms are created or destroyed. So the thing that *changed* between reactants and products is not the atoms — it is the ______________ stored in their bonds.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** A campfire log gives off heat and light as it burns. The log used to be a living tree. Trace and explain the energy.

**Step 1 — Where did the energy in the fire come from?**

The energy released by the fire is ______________ energy that was stored in the bonds of the wood (mostly sugars/cellulose the tree built).

**Step 2 — How did the energy get into the wood in the first place?**

The tree ran ______________: it used ______________ energy from the Sun to build high-energy sugar from low-energy CO₂ and H₂O. The Sun's energy was stored in the bonds.

**Step 3 — Which direction on the energy diagram is *burning*?**

Burning runs the reaction in ______________ (forward / reverse). On the diagram it moves from the ______________ level to the ______________ level, which means energy is ______________ (stored / released).

**Step 4 — Is energy created or destroyed at any step?**

No. By the law of ______________, the energy is only transferred and changed in form: light → chemical → heat and light. The total energy stays the ______________.

**Step 5 — Because / But / So**

The campfire releases heat and light
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name the stored chemical energy and where it came from (the Sun, via photosynthesis); "but" should note that the atoms were only rearranged, never created; "so" should state that burning releases the stored sunlight, with energy conserved.

## Summary

Complete the Because / But / So sentence:

> The products of photosynthesis (sugar + O₂) store more energy than the reactants (CO₂ + H₂O)
> because __________________________________________,
> but __________________________________________,
> so __________________________________________.

**Reminder:** A plant gets its *atoms* from air (CO₂) and water; it gets its *energy* from sunlight. Photosynthesis does not *make* energy — it *stores* the Sun's energy in chemical bonds. Burning or eating the sugar later releases that exact energy. Energy is always conserved.

**Key reference:**
Photosynthesis: 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ (light energy in, stored in bonds)
Reverse (respiration / combustion): C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ → 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O (energy released)
