## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Cause and Effect in the lab:** Every safety rule exists because of a predictable cause→effect chain. The *cause* is the __________ (something with potential to harm). The *effect* is the injury that would follow if no control is in place. PPE and safety procedures __________ that chain before harm reaches the student.

**The SDS — three sections you must know:**

- **Section ___** — Hazard identification: lists hazard statements (H-statements) in plain English.
- **Section ___** — First-aid measures: tells you what to do if exposure happens.
- **Section ___** — Exposure controls / PPE: lists required protective equipment.

**Cause and Effect (CCC connection):**

Complete the sentence:

> Without PPE, the *cause* (a splash, a vapor, a spill) connects directly to the *__________ *, producing an injury.
> With PPE, the cause still happens, but the __________ gear breaks the chain, so the effect is __________.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** A student is working with hydrochloric acid (HCl solution, 1 M). She reads the SDS before starting. Section 2 says: *"Causes serious eye irritation. Harmful if inhaled."* Section 8 says: *"Wear splash-proof goggles and acid-resistant apron. Use in a fume hood."*

4. **Locate the first-aid step:** Section ___ — for eye contact: flush eyes with water for ___ minutes.

5. **Because / But / So:** Working with HCl without goggles is dangerous
   **because** _______________________________________________,
   **but** _______________________________________________,
   **so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name the hazard → effect link; "but" should name the PPE or control; "so" should name how the outcome changes.
