## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Scale, Proportion, and Quantity:** Percent composition tells us each element's __________ of the whole compound mass. The proportions are __________ by the formula — every sample of a given compound has the same percent composition regardless of __________.

**The percent-composition formula:**

% element = ( n × atomic mass of element ) ÷ __________________ × 100

where:
- n = the __________ of that element in the formula
- atomic mass = from the __________
- the denominator = __________ — the total mass of the compound

**Why the denominator is the GFM:** The GFM is the __________ pie. Each element's mass contribution is one __________. Dividing the slice by the whole gives a decimal (the __________); multiplying by 100 converts it to a percent.

**The Hochman appositive definition:**

> Percent composition — _________________________ — is calculated by _________________________.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** A student needs to find the percent composition of each element in CO₂.

**Step 1 — Find the GFM of CO₂:**

| Element | Atomic mass (g/mol) | Subscript | Mass contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| C | ___ | 1 | ___ |
| O | ___ | 2 | ___ |
| **GFM** | | | **___ g/mol** |

**Step 2 — Calculate each element's percent:**

% C = ( ___ × ___ ) ÷ ___ × 100 = ____%

% O = ( ___ × ___ ) ÷ ___ × 100 = ____%

**Step 3 — Check:**

% C + % O = ___% + ___% = ____%   (should equal ___________)

**Step 4 — Appositive sentence:**

The percent carbon in carbon dioxide — ___% — is calculated by dividing ___ g/mol (C's contribution) by ___ g/mol (GFM) and multiplying by 100.

**Step 5 — Because / But / So**

If you used CO instead of CO₂ for this calculation, the percent carbon would change
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should explain what changes in the formula; "but" should name what stays the same (carbon's atomic mass); "so" should compare the two percent-carbon values.
