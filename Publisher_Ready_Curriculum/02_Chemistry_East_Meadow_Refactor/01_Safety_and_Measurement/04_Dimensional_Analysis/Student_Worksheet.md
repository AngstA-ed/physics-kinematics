## Phenomenon

A copper penny has a mass of about 3.1 grams — easy to measure with a lab balance. But that same penny contains roughly 29,000,000,000,000,000,000,000 copper atoms. Nobody has ever counted them one by one. A chemist can calculate that number from the mass in about thirty seconds.

**Driving question:** How do we convert between very large and very small quantities — atoms ↔ grams — using only multiplication and unit cancellation?
