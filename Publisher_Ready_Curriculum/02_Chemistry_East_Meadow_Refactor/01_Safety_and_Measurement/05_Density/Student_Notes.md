## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Structure and Function:** The way particles are __________ at the molecular level determines the __________ of density at the macroscopic level. Density determines whether an object __________ or __________ in water.

**The formula:**

> D = ___ / ___

Units: D is measured in __________ or __________. Mass is measured in __________. Volume is measured in __________.

**Rearranging for mass and volume (formula triangle):**

| Cover the unknown | Formula to use |
|---|---|
| Cover D | D = m ÷ V |
| Cover m | m = D × V |
| Cover V | V = m ÷ D |

**Float vs. sink rule:**

- If D_object ___ 1.0 g/mL → object **floats** in water.
- If D_object ___ 1.0 g/mL → object **sinks** in water.

**The slope connection:**

- x-axis = __________ (mL)
- y-axis = __________ (g)
- Slope = rise / run = Δmass / Δvolume = ___________ (g/mL)

This means: the slope of a mass-vs-volume graph **equals** the __________ of the material.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** A 27.0 g block of aluminum has a volume of 10.0 mL. Find its density and predict whether it sinks or floats in water.

**Step 1 — Identify what you know and what you need:**

- Known: mass = _____ g, volume = _____ mL
- Unknown: density = ?

**Step 2 — Write the formula:**

D = ___ / ___

**Step 3 — Substitute and calculate:**

D = _____ g / _____ mL = _______________  g/mL

**Step 4 — Compare to water:**

D_aluminum = _______________ g/mL    D_water = 1.0 g/mL

Since _______________ g/mL > 1.0 g/mL, this block will __________ in water.

**Step 5 — Because / But / So**

The aluminum block sinks in water
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should give the density comparison; "but" should name the material that does float (something with density < 1.0 g/mL); "so" should state the consequence for the aluminum block.

**Key reference values (from the 2025 Reference Table):**

| Substance | Density (g/mL) |
|---|---|

| Aluminum | 2.70 |

**Reminder:** To identify an unknown material, calculate D = m/V and compare to a reference table. Density is an intensive property — doubling the sample size doubles both mass and volume, leaving D unchanged.
