## Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This opening unit builds the foundational safety routines and quantitative skills — measurement, significant figures, dimensional analysis, density, gram formula mass, percent composition, and fluency with the 2025 NYS Chemistry Reference Tables — that every later HS-PS1 unit depends on. Students move from safe lab habits through increasingly sophisticated mathematical tools, arriving at the end of the unit prepared to apply those tools to any chemistry context.

| # | Lesson | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Lab Safety | Safe-investigation routines; PPE, SDS, hazards break the cause→effect chain |
| 02 | Measurement & SI Units | Measure with correct SI units and instruments; precision vs. accuracy |
| 03 | Significant Figures & Scientific Notation | Record only the digits a measurement justifies; write very large/small numbers compactly |
| 04 | Dimensional Analysis | Convert across scales with conversion factors; units cancel |
| 05 | Density | D = m/V; particle spacing explains why ice floats |
| 06 | Gram Formula Mass | Sum atomic masses to get the mass of one mole |
| 07 | Percent Composition | Each element's share of a compound's mass |
| 08 | Chemistry Reference Tables | Navigate the 2025 NYS Reference Tables fluently; unit synthesis |

## Pacing Calendar

| Day | Lesson | Phenomenon anchor | Strategy |

| 3 | 03 Sig Figs & Scientific Notation | One pencil measured three ways; calculator over-precision | BTC |

| 5 | 05 Density | Ice floats; lakes freeze top-down | Active Learning |

| 7 | 07 Percent Composition | Two fertilizers, different % nitrogen | BTC |

Double-period days (every other day) let the density and percent-composition labs run long.

## Strategy Rotation

- **RESTORATIVE CIRCLE**: Lesson 01 (unit-opening circle).
- **ACTIVE LEARNING**: Lessons 02, 05, 08 (measurement stations; density lab; reference-tables stations).
- **BTC (Building Thinking Classrooms)**: Lessons 03, 07 (sig-fig and percent-composition thin-slices at vertical surfaces).
- **HOCHMAN (literacy)**: Lessons 04, 06 (Because/But/So on unit cancellation; appositive defining gram formula mass).

SEL (opening connection + closing reflection), ELL/SPED supports, OPTIC/CER literacy, and a Curated Resources block appear in every lesson.
