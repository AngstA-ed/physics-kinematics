## Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This unit turns the symbolic language of chemistry into a quantitative tool. Students begin by writing and balancing chemical equations, then ground that procedure in the Law of Conservation of Mass — atoms are rearranged, never created or destroyed. From there they learn to classify reactions by type, to predict whether a single-replacement reaction will occur using the activity series, and finally to count the uncountable: the mole becomes the bridge between the particle scale they cannot see and the gram scale they can weigh. The unit culminates in empirical/molecular formula determination and full mole-ratio stoichiometry, equipping students to answer the central HS-PS1-7 question — *how much?* — for any reaction they encounter.

| # | Lesson | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Writing & Balancing Equations | Adjust coefficients so atoms in = atoms out; the equation records a real rearrangement |
| 02 | Conservation of Matter | Mass is conserved; "lost" or "gained" mass is the system being open vs. closed |
| 03 | Types of Chemical Reactions | Synthesis, decomposition, single/double replacement, combustion — patterns predict products |
| 04 | Spontaneous Reactions | The activity series ranks metals; a more active metal replaces a less active one |
| 05 | Defining the Mole | The mole counts particles by weighing; Avogadro's number links count, mass, and molar mass |
| 06 | Empirical & Molecular Formulas | Mass-percent data yields the simplest atom ratio, then the true molecular formula |
| 07 | Mole Calculations & Stoichiometry | Mole ratios from a balanced equation predict amounts; the limiting reactant caps the product |

## Pacing Calendar

| Day | Lesson | Phenomenon anchor | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01 Writing & Balancing Equations | Magnesium burns sealed — the balance rises, not falls | Active Learning (unit opener) |
| 2 | 02 Conservation of Matter | Steel wool gains mass as it burns; sealed flask holds steady | Hochman |
| 3 | 03 Types of Chemical Reactions | Two colorless solutions: one blooms a white solid, one stays clear | BTC |
| 4 | 04 Spontaneous Reactions | Zinc coats with copper, but copper ignores zinc sulfate | Active Learning |
| 5 | 05 Defining the Mole | Counting 500 beans by weighing, not one at a time | Hochman |
| 6 | 06 Empirical & Molecular Formulas | An unlabeled white powder named "MgO" from two balance readings | BTC |
| 7 | 07 Mole Calculations & Stoichiometry | A fire dies with fuel left — oxygen ran out at the flame front | Active Learning |

Double-period days (every other day) give the formula-determination and stoichiometry labs room to run long.

---

## Strategy Rotation

- **ACTIVE LEARNING**: Lessons 01, 04, 07 (balancing-equation manipulatives and unit-opening circle; activity-series prediction stations; campfire/limiting-reactant lab).
- **HOCHMAN (literacy)**: Lessons 02, 05 (Because/But/So on open vs. closed systems; appositive defining the mole and Avogadro's number).
- **BTC (Building Thinking Classrooms)**: Lessons 03, 06 (reaction-type thin-slices and empirical-formula problem strings at vertical surfaces).
- **RESTORATIVE CIRCLE**: opens Lesson 01 (unit launch) and Lesson 06 (mid-unit re-grounding before formula work).

SEL (opening connection + closing reflection), ELL/SPED supports, and OPTIC/CER literacy appear in every lesson.
