## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Patterns:** Thousands of reactions fall into just five recurring patterns. Once you can read the *pattern* of how the reactants are arranged, you can __________ the products — without memorizing each reaction one at a time.

**The five reaction patterns:**

| Family | Pattern | What happens | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Synthesis | A + B → AB |  | 2 Mg + O₂ → 2 MgO |
| Decomposition | AB → A + B |  | 2 H₂O₂ → 2 H₂O + O₂ |
| Single replacement | A + BC → B + AC |  | Zn + 2 HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂ |
| Double replacement | AB + CD → AD + CB |  | AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃ |
| Combustion | fuel + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O |  | CH₄ + 2 O₂ → CO₂ + 2 H₂O |

**How to tell single vs. double replacement** (the most common mix-up):

- A free **element** + a compound → __________ replacement (one swap).
- **Two compounds** → __________ replacement (two swaps).

**Conservation of matter (Energy and Matter CCC):**

In *every* one of the five types, atoms are __________, never created or destroyed. That is why every balanced equation has the same number of each kind of atom on __________ sides. Count the atoms before and after — they must match.

> The white solid in Cup 1 was not created out of nothing. Its atoms were __________ in the liquids the whole time; the reaction rearranged them into a new combination (AgCl) that could not stay __________, so it appeared as a solid.

## Worked Example

**Classify, then predict.**

**Part A — Classify by pattern.** For each reaction, name the type and the pattern that tells you.

1. 2 H₂ + O₂ → 2 H₂O → type: _______________ (pattern: two substances combine into one product)
2. CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ → type: _______________ (pattern: one compound breaks into simpler substances)
3. Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu → type: _______________ (pattern: a free element swaps with one partner)

**Part B — Predict the products of a double replacement** by swapping partners.

> **Reaction:** BaCl₂ + Na₂SO₄ → ?

1. Identify the partners: Ba²⁺ with Cl⁻, and Na⁺ with SO₄²⁻.
2. **Swap them:** Ba pairs with __________, Na pairs with __________.
3. Write the products with correct formulas: __________ + __________.
4. Decide which product is the solid (precipitate): __________ does not stay dissolved.

*Check:* the products should be BaSO₄ (the white solid) + 2 NaCl (stays dissolved). Count the atoms on each side to confirm matter is conserved.
