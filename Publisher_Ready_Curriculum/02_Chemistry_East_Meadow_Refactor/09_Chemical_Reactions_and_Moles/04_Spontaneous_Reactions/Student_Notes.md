## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Patterns:** A ranked list of metals lets me __________ the outcome of a reaction I have never run, because the same rule applies every time: a metal __________ on the activity series replaces a metal __________ on the activity series from its compound.

**What "active" means:** An active metal loses its __________ electrons easily to become a positive __________. The most active metals sit toward the __________-left of the periodic table, where electronegativity is __________.

**The single-replacement rule:**

| If the free metal is… | …compared to the metal in the solution | Result |
|---|---|---|
| MORE active (higher on Table J) |  | __________ (reaction occurs) |
| LESS active (lower on Table J) |  | __________ (no reaction) |
| the SAME element (its own salt) |  | __________ (nothing to replace) |

**Reading a single-replacement equation:** element + compound → new element + new compound

> Zn + CuSO₄ → __________ + __________

The __________ (more active) goes *into* solution as an ion; the __________ (less active) comes *out* as a free metal.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** Predict whether **magnesium reacts with zinc sulfate solution (ZnSO₄)**. If it does, write and balance the products.

**Step 1 — Find both metals on the activity series.**

The free metal is __________. The metal in the solution is __________.

**Step 2 — Compare positions.**

Magnesium is __________ (above / below) zinc on Table J. Therefore magnesium is __________ (more / less) active than zinc.

**Step 3 — Apply the rule.**

Because the free metal (Mg) is __________ active than the metal in the compound (Zn), a single-replacement reaction __________ (does / does not) occur.

**Step 4 — Write and balance the products.**

Magnesium replaces zinc. Mg becomes Mg²⁺ (so it pairs with SO₄²⁻ as MgSO₄), and zinc comes out as free metal:

> Mg + ZnSO₄ → __________ + __________

**Step 5 — Because / But / So**

Magnesium reacts with zinc sulfate solution
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name Mg's higher position / activity; "but" should note what zinc (the less active metal) cannot do; "so" should state the balanced products and the one-way direction.

## Summary

Complete the rule in one sentence:

> A single-replacement reaction occurs only when the free metal is __________ active than the metal in the compound; the __________ metal replaces the __________ metal, and the reaction runs __________ direction only.

Complete the Because / But / So sentence:

> Copper reacts with silver nitrate but not with zinc sulfate
> because __________________________________________,
> but __________________________________________,
> so __________________________________________.

**Reminder:** Activity is about how easily a metal *loses its valence electrons*, not its density or shine. A metal in a solution of its *own* salt shows no reaction — there is nothing of a different element to replace.

**Key reference (from the 2025 NYS Reference Tables):**
Activity Series of Metals — Reference Table J.
Common ion charges: Mg²⁺, Zn²⁺, Fe²⁺, Cu²⁺, Ag⁺; SO₄²⁻ (sulfate), NO₃⁻ (nitrate).
