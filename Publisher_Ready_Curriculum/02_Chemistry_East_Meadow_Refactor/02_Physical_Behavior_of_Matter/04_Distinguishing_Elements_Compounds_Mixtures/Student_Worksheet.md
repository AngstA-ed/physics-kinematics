## Phenomenon

Sodium is a soft metal that catches fire when it touches water — it has to be stored under oil. Chlorine is a poisonous green-yellow gas. But when one sodium atom *bonds* to one chlorine atom, you get sodium chloride — ordinary table salt — which is safe to eat and which your body needs every day. Same two ingredients. A dangerous metal and a poisonous gas combine into the harmless salt on your french fries.

**Driving question:** Why does table salt taste, look, and behave so differently from the sodium and chlorine that make it?
