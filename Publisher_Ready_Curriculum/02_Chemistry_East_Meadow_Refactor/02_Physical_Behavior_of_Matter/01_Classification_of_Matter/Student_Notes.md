## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Structure and Function:** How a sample is put together at the __________ level decides how we classify it. The first question is always: can the sample be physically __________ into different substances? If it cannot, it is a __________ substance. If it can, it is a __________.

**The two-question classification test:**

1. **Question 1 — Can it be physically separated?**
   - No → it is a __________ substance.
   - Yes → it is a __________.

2. **Question 2a — If it is a pure substance:** is it made of one kind of atom, or two or more kinds bonded?
   - One kind of atom → __________ (e.g. copper, O₂)
   - Two or more kinds of atoms bonded in a fixed ratio → __________ (e.g. H₂O, NaCl)

3. **Question 2b — If it is a mixture:** does it look uniform, or can you see the parts?
   - Uniform all the way through → __________ mixture (e.g. saltwater, air)
   - You can see the separate parts → __________ mixture (e.g. trail mix, sand + iron)

**Prefix hint:** *homo-* means __________ ; *hetero-* means __________ .

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** You are handed a cup of clear, colorless saltwater and a separate jar of clear, colorless distilled water. They look identical. Classify each one.

**Step 1 — Question 1: Can it be physically separated?**

- Saltwater: if you boil it, water vapor escapes and solid __________ is left behind → it CAN be separated → __________ .
- Distilled water: if you boil it, only water vapor leaves and __________ is left behind → it CANNOT be separated → __________ substance.

**Step 2 — Sub-classify each one:**

- Saltwater is a mixture. Does it look uniform or can you see the parts? It looks __________ all the way through → it is a __________ mixture.
- Distilled water is a pure substance. Is it one kind of atom or two kinds bonded? Water is H₂O — __________ kinds of atoms (hydrogen and oxygen) bonded → it is a __________ .

**Step 3 — State the classification:**

- Saltwater = __________________________________________
- Distilled water = __________________________________________

**Step 4 — Because / But / So**

Saltwater and distilled water look identical
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name the shared appearance; "but" should name the separability difference; "so" should state the two different classifications.

**Reminder:** Appearance does not classify matter. A homogeneous mixture can look exactly like a pure substance — the real test is whether the components can be physically separated, which depends on how the particles are put together.
