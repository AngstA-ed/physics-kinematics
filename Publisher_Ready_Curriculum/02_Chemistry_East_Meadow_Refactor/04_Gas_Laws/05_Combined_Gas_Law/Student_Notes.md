## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Scale, Proportion, and Quantity:** For a fixed amount of gas, volume is __________ proportional to absolute temperature and __________ proportional to pressure. When you change two of the three variables, the third must adjust so that the quantity P·V/T stays __________.

**The combined gas law:**

> P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂

State 1 is the gas's __________ condition; state 2 is its __________ condition.

**Three laws in one.** The combined gas law contains the three earlier gas laws as special cases:

- Hold **temperature** constant → the T's cancel → P₁V₁ = P₂V₂  (__________ law)
- Hold **pressure** constant → V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂  (__________ law)
- Hold **volume** constant → P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂  (__________ law)

**Two non-negotiable rules:**

1. Temperature must be in __________ . Convert: K = °C + ______.
2. P₁ and P₂ must use the __________ unit (and V₁, V₂ the same unit). The units then cancel.

**STP — the free reference state.** When a problem says a gas is "at STP," write down:

- P = __________ kPa
- T = __________ K

**The Hochman appositive definition:**

> The combined gas law — _________________________ — relates the pressure, volume, and temperature of a fixed gas sample.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** A gas occupies 2.0 L at STP. Its pressure is doubled to 202.6 kPa and its temperature is doubled to 546 K. Find the new volume V₂.

**Step 1 — Write down the knowns and the unknown.**

State 1 (at STP): P₁ = __________ kPa,  V₁ = __________ L,  T₁ = __________ K

State 2: P₂ = 202.6 kPa,  V₂ = ?,  T₂ = 546 K

**Step 2 — Confirm temperatures are in Kelvin.** Both T₁ and T₂ are already in __________ . ✓

**Step 3 — Write the combined gas law and rearrange for V₂.**

> P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂   →   V₂ = (P₁ × V₁ × T₂) ÷ (T₁ × P₂)

**Step 4 — Substitute.**

> V₂ = (101.3 × 2.0 × 546) ÷ (273 × 202.6)

**Step 5 — Solve.**

> V₂ = __________ ÷ __________ = __________  L

**Step 6 — Sanity check.** The pressure doubled (would halve the volume) but the temperature also doubled (would double the volume). The two effects __________ each other, so the volume stays __________.

**Step 7 — Appositive sentence.**

The combined gas law — ___________________________ — let me solve for the new volume by keeping P·V/T constant.

**Step 8 — Because / But / So**

The volume did not change in this problem
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name what doubling the pressure does; "but" should name what doubling the temperature does; "so" should state that the effects cancel and V₂ = V₁.
