## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Structure and Function:** When the same element is arranged in a different __________, its observable __________ change. The kind of atom stays the same; only the __________ of the atoms is different.

**Allotropes — the rule:**

- Allotropes are different structural forms of the __________ element.
- The atoms are __________ (identical); only how they are bonded or arranged is different.
- Because the structure is different, the __________ (hardness, slipperiness, conductivity) are different.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** Explain, using structure, why diamond is much harder than graphite even though both are pure carbon.

**Step 1 — State what is the same:**

Diamond and graphite are both made of the element __________ — the same atoms, nothing else mixed in.

**Step 2 — Describe each structure:**

- Diamond: each carbon bonds to ___ neighbors in a rigid ___-dimensional network.
- Graphite: each carbon bonds to ___ neighbors in flat __________ that are only weakly stacked.

**Step 3 — Connect structure to property (Cause → Effect):**

- Diamond's 3-D network has no easy direction to slide, so it is extremely __________.
- Graphite's sheets slide past one another, so it is __________ and rubs onto paper.

**Step 4 — Name the relationship:**

Diamond and graphite are __________ of carbon — the same element in different structural forms.

**Step 5 — Because / But / So**

Diamond is harder than graphite
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name the structural difference; "but" should remind us the element is identical; "so" should state that the structure, not the element, causes the hardness difference.
