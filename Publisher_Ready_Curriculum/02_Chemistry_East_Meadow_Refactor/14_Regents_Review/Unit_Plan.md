## Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This closing unit is a single cumulative review that reframes the eleven units of the course as one connected story of matter and energy rather than eleven separate topics to cram. Students build a concept map that links classification, atomic structure, bonding, reactions, and the special cases of solutions, kinetics, and acids/redox; they then practice retrieving values fluently from the 2025 NYS Chemistry Reference Tables and running mixed-topic calculations under time pressure. The work is pure pattern-recognition: every Regents item belongs to a recognizable type (a mole calculation, a Table-S density lookup, a Table-F solubility check, a balancing problem), and the day trains students to name the type and reach for the matching tool, arriving exam-ready in June.

| # | Lesson | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Cumulative Review | Eleven units are one story of matter and energy; recognize a Regents question's *type* and reach for the matching reference-table tool |

## Strategy Rotation

- **RESTORATIVE CIRCLE**: Lesson 01 (unit-opening circle — name one chemistry idea that finally "clicked" and one that still feels shaky).
- **ACTIVE LEARNING**: Lesson 01 (five timed rotating review stations with sealed answer envelopes for instant self-check).
- **BTC (Building Thinking Classrooms)**: not used in this single-lesson review unit.
- **HOCHMAN (literacy)**: embedded in Lesson 01 (Because/But/So expansion and the Pattern connection sentence in the Elaborate phase).

SEL (opening circle + closing reflection), ELL/SPED supports, and OPTIC/CER literacy appear in every lesson.
