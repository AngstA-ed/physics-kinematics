## Worked Example

**Scenario:** A student needs to write and balance the neutralization of sulfuric acid (H₂SO₄) with sodium hydroxide (NaOH).

**Step 1 — Write the reactants:**

H₂SO₄ + NaOH → ?

**Step 2 — Identify the products (salt + water):**

- The H⁺ and OH⁻ form __________ (water).
- The leftover ions are Na⁺ (from the base) and SO₄²⁻ (from the acid). Because SO₄²⁻ has a 2− charge, it needs __________ Na⁺ ions. The salt is __________.

So the unbalanced equation is: H₂SO₄ + NaOH → Na₂SO₄ + H₂O

**Step 3 — Count atoms on each side (unbalanced):**

| Element | Left | Right | Balanced? |
|---|---|---|---|
| H | 3 | 2 | no |
| S | 1 | 1 | yes |
| O | 5 | 5 | check again |
| Na | 1 | 2 | no |

**Step 4 — Add coefficients so atoms match:** The acid donates ___ H⁺, so you need ___ NaOH to supply ___ OH⁻, which makes ___ H₂O.

> _____ H₂SO₄ + _____ NaOH → _____ Na₂SO₄ + _____ H₂O

**Step 5 — Re-count to confirm it is balanced:**

| Element | Left | Right | Balanced? |
|---|---|---|---|
| H | ___ | ___ | ___ |
| S | ___ | ___ | ___ |
| O | ___ | ___ | ___ |
| Na | ___ | ___ | ___ |

**Step 6 — Appositive sentence:**

> The neutralization of sulfuric acid — _________________________ — produces the salt __________ and water.

**Step 7 — Because / But / So**

The neutralization of H₂SO₄ with NaOH needs a coefficient of 2 on the base
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should explain why two OH⁻ are needed; "but" should acknowledge what you would get if you forgot the coefficient; "so" should state the correctly balanced equation.
