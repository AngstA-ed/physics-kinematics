## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Scale, Proportion, and Quantity:** pH packs a huge range of hydrogen-ion concentrations onto a small scale from __________ to __________. Each step on the scale is **not** an addition — it is a __________-fold change in [H⁺]. A __________ pH means a high [H⁺] (acidic); a __________ pH means a low [H⁺] (basic).

**The pH scale — three zones:**

| pH range | Name | [H⁺] compared to OH⁻ |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 6 | __________ | more H⁺ than OH⁻ |
| 7 | __________ | [H⁺] equals [OH⁻] |
| 8 – 14 | __________ | less H⁺ than OH⁻ |

**Connecting pH and [H⁺]:**

To find the hydrogen-ion concentration from a whole-number pH, use:

> [H⁺] = 10⁻ᵖᴴ   →   for pH = 4, [H⁺] = 10⁻⁴ = __________ mol/L

- As pH goes **up**, [H⁺] goes __________ (the solution becomes less acidic).
- As pH goes **down**, [H⁺] goes __________ (the solution becomes more acidic).
- Each whole pH unit changes [H⁺] by a factor of __________.

**The Hochman appositive definition:**

> pH — _________________________ — runs from 0 (most acidic) to 14 (most basic), with 7 as neutral.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** A student measures a cup of black coffee and finds a pH of 5. They need to (1) find its [H⁺], (2) classify it, and (3) compare it to pure water at pH 7.

**Step 1 — Convert pH to [H⁺]:**

pH = 5, so [H⁺] = 10⁻⁵ = ___________ mol/L

**Step 2 — Classify:**

pH 5 is __________ than 7, so the coffee is ___________ (acidic / neutral / basic).

**Step 3 — Compare coffee (pH 5) to water (pH 7):**

Steps from pH 5 to pH 7: 5 → 6 → 7 = ___ steps.
Each step is ×10, so the coffee has 10^___ = ___________ times **more** H⁺ than the water.

**Step 4 — Appositive sentence:**

The coffee's pH — ___________ — tells me it is acidic because its hydrogen-ion concentration is ___________ mol/L.

**Step 5 — Because / But / So**

The coffee is more acidic than pure water
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should compare [H⁺]; "but" should note the count of ×10 steps; "so" should state how many times more acidic.

## Summary

Complete the appositive sentence:

> pH — _________________________ — measures how acidic or basic a solution is on a scale from 0 to 14.

Complete the Because / But / So sentence:

> A solution at pH 2 is far more acidic than a solution at pH 5
> because __________________________________________,
> but __________________________________________,
> so __________________________________________.

**Reminder:** The pH scale is *logarithmic*. Each whole-number step is a **tenfold (×10)** change in hydrogen-ion concentration — so always count the steps and multiply by 10 each time. Subtracting the pH values does **not** give the concentration ratio.

**Key reference (from the 2025 NYS Reference Tables):**
pH = −log[H⁺]  and  [H⁺] = 10⁻ᵖᴴ
Neutral water at 25 °C: [H⁺] = [OH⁻] = 10⁻⁷ mol/L  →  pH 7
