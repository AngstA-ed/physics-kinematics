## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Scale, Proportion, and Quantity:** At the equivalence point, the moles of __________ exactly equal the moles of __________. Neutralization is a __________ relationship: a more concentrated acid needs a __________ volume of the same base to be neutralized.

**The titration procedure — how it works:**

1. Measure a known __________ of the unknown acid into a flask.
2. Add a few drops of __________ (an indicator that is colorless in acid, pink in base).
3. Slowly add a base of __________ concentration from a __________, swirling as you go.
4. Stop at the first single drop that makes the color __________ — this is the endpoint, right at the equivalence point.
5. Read the volume of base delivered, then calculate the unknown concentration.

**The equivalence point:** the moment when moles of base __________ moles of acid, so neither is in excess. On a strong-acid/strong-base curve it sits at pH __________.

**The neutralization equation:**

> M_a V_a = M_b V_b

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| M_a | molarity (concentration) of the __________ |
| V_a | volume of the __________ |
| M_b | molarity (concentration) of the __________ |
| V_b | volume of the __________ |

*Unit note:* the volumes only have to __________ on both sides — mL works as long as both are mL, because the units cancel.

**Solving template (use this for every titration calculation):**

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Label the four numbers: which are M_a, V_a (acid) and which are M_b, V_b (base) |
| 2 | Identify the **one** variable that is missing — that's what you solve for |
| 3 | Substitute the three known values into M_a V_a = M_b V_b |
| 4 | Solve algebraically for the missing variable; keep the unit |

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** 25.0 mL of an unknown HCl is exactly neutralized by 50.0 mL of 0.10 M NaOH. Find the molarity of the acid.

**Step 1 — Label the four numbers:**

Acid: V_a = ______ mL,  M_a = ______ (this is the unknown)
Base: M_b = ______ M,  V_b = ______ mL

**Step 2 — Identify the missing variable:** we are solving for ______.

**Step 3 — Substitute into M_a V_a = M_b V_b:**

> M_a × ______ = ______ × ______

**Step 4 — Solve for M_a (divide both sides by V_a):**

> M_a = ( ______ × ______ ) ÷ ______ = ______ M

**Step 5 — Because / But / So**

This acid came out *more concentrated* than a 0.10 M acid
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should explain why more base was needed; "but" should note that the base concentration was the same in both; "so" should state the acid's calculated molarity.
