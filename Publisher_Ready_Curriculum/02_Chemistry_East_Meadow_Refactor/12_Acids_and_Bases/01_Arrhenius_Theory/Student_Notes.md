## Worked Example

**Scenario:** You are given three labeled bottles and must classify each one from its formula alone, without testing: **HBr**, **Ca(OH)₂**, and **CaCl₂**.

**Step 2 — Apply the rule:**

**Step 3 — Name the ion responsible for the behavior:**

- The acid's properties come from the __________ ion.
- The base's properties come from the __________ ion.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name how the substances differ; "but" should name the ion they share (H⁺); "so" should state that the shared properties come from the shared ion.

> because __________________________________________,

**Reminder — the formula rule:**
- Leading **H** (or –COOH) → **acid** → produces **H⁺**
- **OH** bonded to a metal → **base** → produces **OH⁻**
- Neither → **neutral**

**Key idea:** The properties of an acid or base come from the *ion it releases in water* (H⁺ or OH⁻), not from the parts of the formula that change from one substance to the next. That shared ion is the *pattern* behind the shared behavior.
