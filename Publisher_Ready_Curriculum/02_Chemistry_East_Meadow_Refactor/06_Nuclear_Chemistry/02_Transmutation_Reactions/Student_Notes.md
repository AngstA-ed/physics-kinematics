## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Energy and Matter:** In every nuclear change, the total __________ number and total __________ number are conserved (matter is rearranged, not created or destroyed), while a large amount of __________ is released from the nucleus.

**Two conservation rules for balancing nuclear equations:**

1. The sum of the **mass numbers** (the __________ numbers) must be equal on both sides of the arrow.
2. The sum of the **atomic numbers** (the __________ numbers) must be equal on both sides of the arrow.

A neutron, ¹₀n, has mass number ___ and atomic number ___.
A beta particle, ⁰₋₁e, has mass number ___ and atomic number ___.
An alpha particle, ⁴₂He, has mass number ___ and atomic number ___.

**Two directions a nucleus can change:**

| Process | What happens to the nuclei | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Fission** | one __________ nucleus splits into __________ fragments | U-235 + neutron → Ba + Kr + neutrons |
| **Fusion** | two __________ nuclei merge into __________ larger nucleus | H-2 + H-3 → He-4 + neutron |

Both fission and fusion __________ energy.

**Natural vs. artificial transmutation:**

- **Natural** transmutation happens __________ (on its own) — spontaneous radioactive decay. No outside particle is needed.
- **Artificial** transmutation is forced by __________ the nucleus with a particle (for example, the neutron that starts U-235 fission).

**Is it a type of fission?**

- Alpha emission **is** a type of fission, because a __________ (⁴₂He) splits off the nucleus.
- Beta emission is **not** fission — a __________ inside the nucleus converts into a proton; nothing splits off.
- Gamma emission is **not** fission — only __________ leaves the nucleus; the mass number and atomic number do not change.

**The Hochman appositive definition:**

> Transmutation — _________________________ — always conserves the total mass number and the total atomic number.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** Find the missing nucleus in the alpha decay of uranium-238.

> ²³⁸₉₂U → ___ + ⁴₂He

**Step 1 — Write down what you know.** The alpha particle ⁴₂He has mass number ___ and atomic number ___.

**Step 2 — Balance the mass numbers (top).**

Left top = 238. Right known top = 4 (from He). Missing top = 238 − 4 = ___________

**Step 3 — Balance the atomic numbers (bottom).**

Left bottom = 92. Right known bottom = 2 (from He). Missing bottom = 92 − 2 = ___________

**Step 4 — Identify the element.** Atomic number ___ on the Periodic Table is the element __________ (symbol ___).

**Step 5 — Write the balanced equation:**

> ²³⁸₉₂U → __________ + ⁴₂He

**Step 6 — Classify it.** This decay happens spontaneously, so it is __________ transmutation. Because a fragment (the alpha particle) splits off, alpha decay is a type of __________.

**Step 7 — Because / But / So**

The atomic number of the product is lower than uranium's
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should explain that the alpha particle carried away 2 protons; "but" should note that the total proton count is still conserved across the whole equation; "so" should state the new element's identity.
