## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Structure and Function:** The way a molecule is *built* (its structure — which atoms bond, and by how many pairs) determines how the molecule *behaves* (its function — its properties). Water's strange behavior comes straight from how its atoms are bonded.

**Two kinds of bonds — compare:**

| | Ionic bond | Covalent bond |
|---|---|---|
| What happens to electrons | transferred (given away) | __________ (shared) |
| Which elements | metal + nonmetal | __________ + __________ |
| Result | charged ions attract | atoms held in a __________ |

**Sharing electron pairs — bond order:**

| Bond | Lines drawn | Pairs shared | Electrons shared |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single | — | ___ | ___ |
| Double | = | ___ | ___ |
| Triple | ≡ | ___ | ___ |

> The more pairs two atoms share, the __________ and __________ the bond (stronger / shorter).

**Structural formulas — each line = one shared pair:**

| Molecule | Bonds | Structural formula (fill in the bonds) |
|---|---|---|
| H₂O | 2 single | H — O — H  (drawn **bent**) |
| CH₄ | 4 single | C in center, ___ lines to H |
| NH₃ | 3 single | N in center, ___ lines to H |
| CO₂ | 2 double | O ___ C ___ O |

**Carbon is special (organic chemistry):**

- Carbon almost always makes exactly ___ bonds.
- Carbon can bond **to itself**, forming __________ — the backbone of fuels, plastics, and living things.
- Example: two carbons joined, with hydrogens filling the rest, gives __________ (C₂H₆).

## Worked Example

**Build the structural formula of carbon dioxide, CO₂.**

1. **Count the atoms:** one __________ atom, two __________ atoms.

2. **Place the central atom:** the single carbon goes in the __________; the two oxygens go on either side.

3. **Decide the bond order:** a single line between C and each O does *not* satisfy carbon's need for four bonds. To reach four bonds total, carbon forms a __________ bond to each oxygen.

4. **Count the bonds on carbon:** 2 double bonds = 2 + 2 = ___ bonds total. ✓ (Carbon is satisfied at four.)

5. **Draw it:** O = C = O. Each "=" is a __________ bond — ___ shared pairs, ___ electrons.

6. **Appositive sentence (Hochman):**
   A double bond — __________ shared pairs of electrons between two atoms — is found in carbon dioxide.

*Check your reasoning:* the number of lines on the carbon should add up to **four** (count a double bond as two). If it doesn't, recount.

## Summary

Finish the sentence (Because / But / So) connecting **structure** to **function**:

> Water has unusual properties (like high surface tension or floating ice)
> because its bent structure makes water molecules _______________________________________________,
> but _______________________________________________,
> so _______________________________________________.

**One-line takeaway:** A covalent bond is a *shared* pair of electrons; the number of shared pairs (the bond order) and the molecule's shape make up its structure — and that structure determines how the substance behaves.
