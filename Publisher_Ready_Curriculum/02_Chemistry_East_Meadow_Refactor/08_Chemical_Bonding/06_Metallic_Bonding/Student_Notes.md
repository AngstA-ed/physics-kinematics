|---|---|

| Luster (shine) | Mobile surface electrons __________ light back to your eye. |

## Summary

Complete the Because / But / So sentence:

> A copper wire can be bent into any shape, but a salt crystal shatters when struck,
> because __________________________________________,
> but __________________________________________,
> so __________________________________________.

**Reminder:** In a metal, the valence electrons are **delocalized** — they belong to the whole piece, not to one atom. That mobile "sea" of electrons is the single idea that explains conductivity, malleability, ductility, and luster.
