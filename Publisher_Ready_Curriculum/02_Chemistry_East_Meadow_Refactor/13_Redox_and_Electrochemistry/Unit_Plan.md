## Unit Scope (from East Meadow Scope & Sequence)

This unit develops the electron-transfer story of chemistry from bookkeeping to working device. Students begin by assigning oxidation numbers — the fixed-rule charge labels that make electron movement trackable — then use changes in those numbers to identify which species is oxidized and which is reduced. From there they record the transfer in symbols by splitting a redox reaction into balanced half-reactions, enforcing conservation of charge. The unit lands on the electrochemical cell, where an invisible electron transfer is routed through a wire to produce measurable electricity — the chemistry behind every battery. Each lesson builds the tool the next one needs, so oxidation-number fluency in Lesson 01 ultimately powers the voltaic-cell analysis in Lesson 04.

| # | Lesson | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Assigning Oxidation Numbers | A short fixed rule set assigns a signed charge label to every atom; a change in that label signals redox |
| 02 | Identifying Reduction & Oxidation | A rise in oxidation number = oxidation (loss of electrons); a fall = reduction (gain); electrons are conserved |
| 03 | Writing Half-Reactions | Split a redox reaction into two half-reactions and balance the electrons so loss = gain |
| 04 | Electrochemical Cells | Spontaneous redox routed through a wire makes a voltaic cell; an external source drives an electrolytic cell |

## Strategy Rotation

- **RESTORATIVE CIRCLE**: Lesson 01 (unit-opening community circle on slow, almost-unnoticed change).
- **ACTIVE LEARNING**: Lessons 01, 04 (students assign oxidation numbers before the rule is named; students build a real Cu/Zn cell before *anode*, *cathode*, *voltaic*, and *electrolytic* are spoken).
- **BTC (Building Thinking Classrooms)**: Lesson 03 (random groups split and balance three thin-slice reactions at vertical surfaces, inventing the least-common-multiple fix before it is named).
- **HOCHMAN (literacy)**: Lesson 02 (appositive sentences keep *oxidation = loss/up* and *reduction = gain/down* from drifting apart).

SEL (opening connection circle + closing reflection), ELL/SPED supports, OPTIC/CER literacy, and a Curated Resources block appear in **every** lesson.
