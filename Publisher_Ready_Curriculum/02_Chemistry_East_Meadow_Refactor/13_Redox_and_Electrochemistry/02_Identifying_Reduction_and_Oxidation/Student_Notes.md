## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Energy and Matter (electrons are conserved):** In a redox reaction, electrons are not created or destroyed — they __________ from one species to another. The electrons one species __________ are exactly the electrons another species __________. That is why oxidation and reduction always happen __________.

**The two events — fill in the directions:**

| Event | What happens to electrons | What happens to the oxidation number | Mnemonic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Oxidation** | electrons are __________ | goes __________ (more positive) | **O**xidation **I**s **L**oss / **LE**O |
| **Reduction** | electrons are __________ | goes __________ (more negative) | **R**eduction **I**s **G**ain / **GE**R |

**How to identify oxidation and reduction in a reaction:**

1. Assign the __________ number to each element in the reactants and products (Lesson 01 rules).
2. Find the element whose number went __________ — that species was **oxidized** (it lost electrons).
3. Find the element whose number went __________ — that species was **reduced** (it gained electrons).
4. Check: the electrons __________ must equal the electrons __________ (conservation).

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** Identify the species oxidized and the species reduced in the reaction:

> **2 Al + 3 Cl₂ → 2 AlCl₃**   (in AlCl₃, aluminum is +3 and chlorine is −1)

**Step 1 — Assign oxidation numbers (before and after):**

- Al as a free element = ___    →   Al in AlCl₃ = ___
- Cl as a free element (Cl₂) = ___   →   Cl in AlCl₃ = ___

**Step 2 — Track the direction of each change:**

| Element | Before | After | Up or down? | Lost or gained e⁻? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Al | ___ | ___ | ___ | ___ |
| Cl | ___ | ___ | ___ | ___ |

**Step 3 — Name the events:**

- Aluminum went __________ (0 → +3), so aluminum was __________ . It __________ electrons.
- Chlorine went __________ (0 → −1), so chlorine was __________ . It __________ electrons.

**Step 4 — Appositive sentence:**

In this reaction, aluminum — __________ — is oxidized because its oxidation number goes from ___ to ___.

**Step 5 — Because / But / So**

Aluminum is the species that was oxidized
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name the up/down change; "but" should acknowledge the trap (a positive charge does NOT mean electrons were gained); "so" should state that aluminum lost electrons and was oxidized.

## Summary

Complete the appositive sentences:

> Oxidation — _________________________ — makes the oxidation number go up.
> Reduction — _________________________ — makes the oxidation number go down.

Complete the Because / But / So sentence:

> An iron nail rusts faster in salt water than in fresh water
> because __________________________________________,
> but __________________________________________,
> so __________________________________________.

**Reminder — don't fall for the charge trap:** A species becoming **positive** (like Fe²⁺) *lost* electrons and was **oxidized**. A positive oxidation number does NOT mean electrons were gained. Track the *direction* of the change: **up = lost = oxidized; down = gained = reduced.**

**Key reference (mnemonics):**
**OIL RIG** — Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)
**LEO the lion says GER** — Lose Electrons = Oxidation, Gain Electrons = Reduction
