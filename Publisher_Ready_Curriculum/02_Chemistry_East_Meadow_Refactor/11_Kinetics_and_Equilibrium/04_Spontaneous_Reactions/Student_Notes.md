## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Energy and Matter:** Some changes happen all by themselves; their opposites never do. A change that proceeds on its own, with no outside force, is called __________. Nature tends toward states of __________ energy and __________ disorder.

**"Spontaneous" does NOT mean fast.** Spontaneous tells you the __________ a change goes, not its __________. Rust is spontaneous but takes years.

**The two factors that decide spontaneity:**

| Factor | Favors spontaneity when… | The word for it |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | the process __________ energy (exothermic, ΔH < 0) | __________ |
| Disorder | the disorder __________ (ΔS > 0) | __________ |

**When the two factors agree**, the answer is easy. When they **disagree**, __________ decides which factor wins (heat favors disorder; cold favors order).

**The four-corner grid — fill in each corner from the figure:**

| | Entropy INCREASES (more disorder) | Entropy DECREASES (more order) |
|---|---|---|
| **Exothermic** (releases energy) | __________ spontaneous | depends on __________ |
| **Endothermic** (absorbs energy) | depends on __________ | __________ spontaneous |

**Where does the messy room go on this grid?** Tidying a room absorbs effort (energy in) AND decreases disorder — so it lands in the __________ corner. That is why a room never tidies itself.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** Decide whether **wood burning** is spontaneous, and explain using both factors.

**Step 1 — Describe the energy change.**

When wood burns, you feel heat coming off the fire. So the process __________ energy. This makes it ____________________ (exothermic / endothermic).
The enthalpy factor: ____________________ (favors / does not favor) spontaneity.

**Step 2 — Describe the disorder (entropy) change.**

A solid log turns into spreading hot gases, smoke, and ash. The disorder ____________________ (increases / decreases). So entropy ____________________.
The entropy factor: ____________________ (favors / does not favor) spontaneity.

**Step 3 — Combine the two factors.**

Both factors __________ spontaneity (they agree). So wood burning lands in the __________ corner of the grid: __________ spontaneous.

**Step 4 — Speed check.**

Is "spontaneous" the same as "fast"? __________. Wood needs a spark to *start* (that is activation energy from the rates lesson), but once lit it keeps going on its own — that is what spontaneous means.

**Step 5 — Because / But / So**

Wood burning is spontaneous
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name both factors (releases energy AND increases disorder); "but" should address speed or the spark needed to start; "so" should state that it keeps going on its own.

> A messy room forms on its own, but a tidy room never does,

**Reminders:**

- Spontaneous = happens on its own (a question of direction), **not** fast (that is rate/kinetics).
- Two factors favor spontaneity: **releasing energy** (exothermic) and **increasing entropy** (disorder).
- When the factors disagree, **temperature** decides — heat favors disorder, cold favors order.
