## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Stability and Change:** A system at equilibrium looks ______________ on the outside (constant color, concentration, pressure), but at the particle scale it is still ______________ — molecules keep reacting in both directions. The outside stays steady *because* two opposite changes happen at ______________ rates.

**The double arrow:**

- A single arrow (→) means the reaction goes ______________ (one way / both ways) to completion.
- A double arrow (⇌) means the reaction is ______________ — it runs in both directions at once.

**The TWO characteristics of a system at equilibrium:**

1. The ______________ reaction rate equals the ______________ reaction rate.
2. The ______________ of all reactants and products stay ______________ (do not change).

**Two warnings (the most common mistakes):**

- Equilibrium does **not** mean the reaction has ______________. (It is still running both ways — that's why we call it *dynamic*.)
- "Equal rates" does **not** mean "equal ______________." The amounts can be very different; only the rates are equal.

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** A pair runs the cup-transfer simulation. They start with 40 counters in the Reactants cup and 0 in Products. Each round they move ½ of Reactants forward and 1/10 of Products back, then count.

**Step 1 — Run the first two rounds (model):**

| Round | Reactants (R) | Forward (½ of R) | Products (P) | Back (1/10 of P) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start | 40 | — | 0 | — |
| 1 | _____ | 20 | _____ | 0 |
| 2 | _____ | _____ | _____ | _____ |

*(Round 1: forward = ½·40 = 20, back = 1/10·0 = 0, so R = 40 − 20 + 0 = 20 and P = 0 + 20 − 0 = 20.)*

**Step 2 — Where does it settle?** After several rounds the counts stop changing near:

Reactants ≈ _______   Products ≈ _______   (NOT equal to each other)

**Step 3 — What is equal at the steady state?**

The number of counters moving ______________ each round equals the number moving ______________ each round. The ______________ are equal; the ______________ in the two cups are not.

**Step 4 — Name it:** This steady state is called ______________ ______________. The counters never stop moving — so it is ______________, not stopped.

**Step 5 — Appositive sentence:**

Dynamic equilibrium — the condition where the forward and reverse reactions occur at ______________ rates so the ______________ stay constant — is not a stopped reaction.

**Step 6 — Because / But / So**

The cup counts stopped changing
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name the equal forward/back transfers; "but" should note that counters still move every round; "so" should state that the counts hold constant while the reaction stays dynamic.

**Reminder:** At equilibrium, what is equal is the **rates**, not the **amounts**. The concentrations are **constant** (unchanging) — but they are usually NOT equal to each other. And the reaction never stops; it is **dynamic**.
