## Guided Notes

**The big idea — Energy and Matter:** Energy is never created or destroyed. In a reaction, energy moves between the chemical system (the __________) and the surroundings (the water, the beaker, your hand). When heat flows **out** of the system, the surroundings get __________. When heat flows **into** the system, the surroundings get __________.

**The two-step rule for bonds:**

- Breaking bonds __________ energy (it *costs* energy to pull atoms apart).
- Forming bonds __________ energy (it *pays back* energy when atoms join).

**How to tell exothermic from endothermic:**

| Compare the two totals | Net energy | Products vs. reactants | Surroundings | Name |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forming releases **more** than breaking absorbs | released | products are __________ | get __________ | __________thermic |
| Breaking absorbs **more** than forming releases | absorbed | products are __________ | get __________ | __________thermic |

**Activation energy:** the energy needed to __________ a reaction — the height of the __________ on the diagram. It is present in **both** exothermic and endothermic reactions. (That is why a hand warmer must be __________ before it heats up.)

## Worked Example

**Scenario:** A student needs to decide whether the burning of hydrogen is exothermic or endothermic.

> **2 H₂ + O₂ → 2 H₂O**

Bond energies (kJ per mole of bonds): H–H = 436, O=O = 498, O–H = 463.

**Step 1 — Count the bonds.**

Reactant bonds broken: **2** H–H bonds and **1** O=O bond.
Product bonds formed: **4** O–H bonds (each H₂O has 2 O–H bonds, and there are 2 H₂O molecules).

**Step 2 — Energy absorbed breaking reactant bonds (this *costs* energy):**

2 × (H–H) + 1 × (O=O) = 2 × ____ + ____ = __________ kJ absorbed

**Step 3 — Energy released forming product bonds (this *pays back* energy):**

4 × (O–H) = 4 × ____ = __________ kJ released

**Step 4 — Net energy change (released − absorbed):**

____ − ____ = __________ kJ

**Step 5 — Classify.** Because forming the product bonds released __________ energy than breaking the reactant bonds absorbed, the net energy is released, so the reaction is __________thermic and its surroundings get __________.

**Step 6 — Appositive sentence:**

The burning of hydrogen is an exothermic reaction — _________________________ — so the leftover energy flows out to the surroundings as heat.

**Step 7 — Because / But / So**

When hydrogen burns, the surroundings get warmer
**because** _______________________________________________,
**but** _______________________________________________,
**so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name which bond-energy total is larger; "but" should note that the products store less energy than the reactants; "so" should state that the leftover energy flows out as heat.

Complete the Because / But / So sentence:

> When you squeeze an instant cold pack, your hand gets cold
> because __________________________________________,
> but __________________________________________,

**Reminder:** Breaking bonds *absorbs* energy; forming bonds *releases* energy. A reaction is exothermic when forming releases **more** than breaking absorbed (products lower, surroundings warm). It is endothermic when breaking absorbs **more** than forming releases (products higher, surroundings cool). Energy is always **conserved** — it just moves between the system and the surroundings.

**Key reference (from the 2025 NYS Reference Tables):**
Energy unit: kilojoule (kJ). Reaction-coordinate (potential-energy) diagrams show the relative potential energies of reactants and products and the activation energy.
