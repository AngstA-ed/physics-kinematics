## Worked Example

**Scenario:** A lithium-7 atom has **3 protons, 4 neutrons, and 3 electrons.** Use the charge, mass, and location of each particle to describe the atom.

**Work through the properties:**

1. **Charge of the nucleus:** The nucleus holds the protons and neutrons. Protons: 3 × (+1) = +3. Neutrons: 4 × (0) = 0. Nucleus charge = __________.

2. **Overall charge of the atom:** Nucleus (+3) plus 3 electrons (3 × −1 = −3) = __________. (A neutral atom adds up to zero.)

3. **Where is the mass?** Protons (3 × ≈ 1 = ≈ 3) and neutrons (4 × ≈ 1 = ≈ 4) give a relative mass of about __________ in the nucleus. The 3 electrons add only about 3 × 1/1836 ≈ __________ . So almost all the mass is in the __________.

4. **Where is the volume?** The electrons occupy the __________ around the nucleus — almost all of the atom's volume — even though they carry almost no __________.

5. **Because / But / So:** Lithium is almost entirely empty space
   **because** _______________________________________________,
   **but** _______________________________________________,
   **so** _______________________________________________.

*Check your reasoning:* "because" should name where the electrons are; "but" should name where the protons and neutrons (the mass) are; "so" should explain how the mass concentrates in a tiny region.
