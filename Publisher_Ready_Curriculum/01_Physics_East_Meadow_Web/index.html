Unit · Kinematics · 9 lessons · ~2 weeks

The language of motion — vectors, velocity, acceleration, projectiles.

Nine phenomenon-based lessons aligned to NYSSLS HS-PS2-1, built for the joint East Meadow / Valley Stream Central pilot. Every lesson starts with a real phenomenon, gives students hands-on tools to investigate it, and ends with a transfer task they have to solve themselves.

9 lessons Unit at a glance

Click any lesson to launch its interactive student page. Each lesson is self-contained — drag, slide, predict, and check. Teacher guides and answer keys sit in the OneNote-compatible track at ../01_Physics_East_Meadow_Refactor/01_Kinematics/.

  1. Lesson 01
    Vectors
    Drag-add vectors on a grid. Watch the resultant update live with magnitude and direction readouts.
    HS-PS2-1RESTORATIVE CIRCLE · ACTIVE LEARNING
  2. Lesson 02
    Distance and Displacement
    Build a path step-by-step. Toggle distance and displacement views; round-trip exposes a zero-displacement walk.
    HS-PS2-1
  3. Lesson 03
    Average Speed and Velocity
    Two cars, same start and end, different paths. Race them and compare average speed vs. average velocity.
    HS-PS2-1HOCHMAN
  4. Lesson 04
    Acceleration
    Slide acceleration from −4 to +4 m/s². Three linked graphs (x-t, v-t, a-t) update in real time.
    HS-PS2-1BTC
  5. Lesson 05
    Motion Graphs
    Pick a scenario, watch the dot move, and see x-t and v-t draw alongside. Read the slope.
    HS-PS2-1ACTIVE LEARNING
  6. Lesson 06
    Freefall
    Drop a ball from any height. Toggle air resistance to see why a feather isn't quite freefall.
    HS-PS2-1HOCHMAN
  7. Lesson 07
    Vertical Projectiles
    Throw a ball straight up. Time-of-flight, peak height, and velocity are all symmetric — see for yourself.
    HS-PS2-1
  8. Lesson 08
    Horizontal Projectile Motion
    Roll a marble off a table while another simply drops. Both hit the floor at the same time. Why?
    HS-PS2-1BTC · HOCHMAN
  9. Lesson 09
    Projectiles at an Angle
    Aim and fire. Find the optimal launch angle for max range. Hit the moving target to win.
    HS-PS2-1ACTIVE LEARNING

Backbone Every lesson, every time

Each lesson follows the NYSSLS phenomenon-based lesson flow: phenomenon → notice and wonder → initial model → investigation → sense-making → consensus → vocabulary (≤ 3 terms in the second half) → revise model → exit ticket.

SEL bookends (Opening Connection · Closing Reflection) and Access & Differentiation supports (sentence frames, word-choice boxes, IEP/SPED scaffolds, extensions) appear in every Teacher Guide.
