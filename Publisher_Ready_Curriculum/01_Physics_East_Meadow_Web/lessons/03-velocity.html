Lesson 03 · Unit: Kinematics

Average speed and velocity — same trip, different stories.

Two cars start and end at the same place at the same time. One has higher average speed. The other has higher average velocity. Both can be true. Direction is doing the work.

Hochman · sentence expansion HS-PS2-1 thread

01 Phenomenon

Two students start side-by-side. One walks straight to the door. The other walks halfway, doubles back to a friend's seat, then heads to the door. Both finish at the same time. Same speed? Same velocity?

02 Driving Question

How can two trips have the same average speed but different average velocities — or vice versa?

03 Notice & Wonder

Did the two students "move the same"? Defend your answer with a number — even a guess.

04 Initial Model

Write a kernel sentence: "The car moves." We will expand it together below.

06 Make it make sense

  1. Car A travels 100 m east in 5 s. Car B travels 60 m east, then 40 m west, in 5 s. Average speed and velocity for each?
  2. Use a Hochman expansion: kernel "The car moves." → +speed → +direction → +time. End with a sentence precise enough that another physicist could re-create the motion.
  3. Sketch a scenario where two trips have equal average speeds but different average velocities.
Hochman organizer
  1. Kernel: The car moves.
  2. + speed: The car moves at ___ m/s.
  3. + direction → velocity: The car moves ___ at ___ m/s.
  4. + time + trip detail: The ___ car moves ___ at an average velocity of ___ m/s for ___ seconds.

07 Key vocabulary

speed
Distance ÷ time. A scalar — always ≥ 0.
velocity
Displacement ÷ time. A vector — has direction.
average rate
Total change ÷ total time elapsed.

08 Revise your model

Take "The car moves" through the four expansions, ending with a sentence precise enough to describe Car B's motion from the simulator.

09 Return to the phenomenon

Re-classify the two students from the start of class. Same speed? Same velocity? Use today's vocabulary.

10 Exit ticket + closing reflection

  1. A cyclist rides 20 km along a trail and returns to the start in 1 hour. (a) Average speed? (b) Average velocity? (c) Hochman expansion starting from "The cyclist rode."
  2. Reflection: What surprised you? Who helped?
