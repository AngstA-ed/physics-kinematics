Lesson 04 · Unit: Kinematics

Acceleration — when velocity is changing.

Three graphs, one motion. Drag the acceleration slider and watch position, velocity, and acceleration tell the same story in three different shapes.

Building Thinking Classrooms HS-PS2-1

01 Phenomenon

Open the Physics Classroom acceleration animations. The dots are evenly spaced in time; their spacing on the page changes as the object speeds up or slows down. That spacing is the visible signature of acceleration.

02 Driving Question

How can we measure how fast an object's velocity is changing?

03 Notice & Wonder

Some animations show dots that get further apart over time. What does that mean about the object?

04 Initial Model

Sketch a v-t graph for "speeding up." Then sketch one for "constant velocity." What's the difference?

05 Investigate — three linked graphs

Pick a, then press Run. Watch the dot, then watch all three graphs draw simultaneously. Slope of x-t = velocity. Slope of v-t = acceleration. The a-t graph is the constant you set.

Two cars have the same acceleration but different starting velocities. What's the same about their v-t graphs? What's different?

06 Make it make sense

  1. Set a = +3 m/s² and v₀ = 0. Press Run. Velocity at t = 4 s? Distance traveled?
  2. What does the slope of the v-t graph tell you? Sketch v-t for a car at +2 m/s² for 5 s, then 0 m/s² for 3 s.
  3. A car moving west has a negative acceleration (eastward-positive convention). Speeding up or slowing down? Explain.

07 Key vocabulary

acceleration
Change in velocity ÷ time. A vector. Units m/s².
deceleration
Acceleration whose vector opposes the velocity (slowing down).
slope
Steepness of the v-t graph; equals the acceleration.

08 Revise your model

Update your initial v-t sketches. Add slope labels. Use the word acceleration.

09 Return to the phenomenon

Reopen one of the Physics Classroom animations. Predict its v-t graph, then check yourself with the simulator.

10 Exit ticket + closing reflection

  1. A sprinter starts from rest and reaches 9 m/s in 3 s. (a) Average a? (b) v-t sketch. (c) Slows from 9 to 0 in 6 s — what is a during the slow-down? Sign? Why?
  2. Reflection: What surprised you? Who helped?
